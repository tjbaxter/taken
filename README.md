# Taken

Did I take it? Know for sure.

Taken is a source of truth for a daily pill and supplement stack. Tap when taken. Look later and see it, with the exact time. No accounts, no ads, no tracking, no network calls of its own. Everything stays on the iPhone.

- One tap logs a dose from the app, the Home Screen widget, the Lock Screen, Control Center, the evening nudge or Siri.
- Three tabs (1.1): Today (the stack, one calm progress line, a + beside it), To-do (voice to-dos) and History (five weeks, streak and 30-day adherence; backfill any day). The avatar on each tab opens Profile: a name and a photo if you want them, and every setting.
- Adding a stack needs no typing: quick-pick chips (27, Birth Control, Semaglutide and Tirzepatide among them), how often each is taken, done. Typing is optional: + CUSTOM opens a field pinned under the header with type-ahead over a bundled list of 299 names, typo tolerant ("tum" finds Turmeric), on the iPhone with no lookup.
- Daily, every other day, weekly on a chosen day (a weekly dose counts for its week, even logged days after), 2x and 3x daily, or chosen weekdays. A wake-up time keeps a 1 a.m. dose on the right day.
- Voice to-dos (1.1): one tap on the mic, say what needs doing in your own messy words, and Taken turns it into tasks with times, on the iPhone (SpeechAnalyzer or on-device SFSpeechRecognizer, then Apple Foundation Models where Apple Intelligence runs). Siri, the Action button, Control Center, the Lock Screen and the app icon start a note. Code checks every task against the words said and joins one action the model split (a shop or website said after it, a list of things, a lone word), and Keep as one puts a split note back together with one tap. One list, as in 1.1 before the Personal and Work lists (feedback build 3: choosing a list for each note was decision fatigue), with Done today under it, and the original Listening card: the words as they are said, no waveform and no timer, and only from 4:00 a quiet "1 minute left". A clock time is kept only when one was said for that task; a day alone keeps the day. Talk for up to 5 minutes; a long note is sorted in parts while you talk, and whatever the model cannot sort is saved as you said it, so no word is lost. The first note needs nothing; after it, voice to-dos are part of Taken Pro.
- Shortcuts (1.1): "Did I take it?" returns its answer as a value, and "Get to-dos" and "Add to-do" read and add to the list, so a shortcut can hand them to Ask Claude, Ask ChatGPT or Use Model. Taken itself sends nothing.
- Four looks in Profile: Signal (amber on black, the default), Blush (light, rose on warm off-white), Phosphor (green) and Ice (cyan). The widgets, the Control Center control and the app icon follow the look.

- Taken Pro (1.1): the first dose needs nothing; logging after it needs Pro: yearly (7 days on us for new subscribers), monthly or lifetime through StoreKit 2. Everyone whose first download was 1.0 keeps Pro for life. The log, history and CSV export always stay readable.

## 1.1 retention build (2026-10-21)

Tom asked what people would enjoy most, what has the highest expected value and what keeps them renewing ("Base it off that"), and that Taken stay a global app, not a UK one. Four levers, each small, none in the capture path and none with an ad (SPEC 3.11, REDESIGN.md "1.1 retention build"; own choices are marked there):
- A weekly dose counts for its week: logged up to 6 days after its day it counts for that day at its real time, so the streak holds; an open week counts neither way; a dose 1 to 3 days early covers the next due day (the 3 days is our own choice). No countdown and no dosing advice, since the labels' windows differ (https://www.novo-pi.com/ozempic.pdf, https://pi.lilly.com/us/zepbound-uspi.pdf); broken streaks cost later use (https://www.colorado.edu/business/faculty-research/2023/04/19/or-track-how-broken-streaks-affect-consumer-decisions).
- The evening nudge, offered once on Today after the stack is added ("Nudge me at 20:00 if anything's left", Turn on or Not now, and Profile's Nudge toggle counts as an answer too); off by default still. Reminders doubled the odds of taking medication across 16 trials (https://research.bond.edu.au/en/publications/mobile-telephone-text-messaging-for-medication-adherence-in-chron/), and Apple says to ask in context (https://developer.apple.com/documentation/usernotifications/asking-permission-to-use-notifications).
- The widget invite, once, under the first All taken, opening a short how-to sheet, and never for someone whose Lock Screen already has the Taken widget (https://blog.duolingo.com/widget-feature, https://support.apple.com/en-us/118610, https://developer.apple.com/documentation/widgetkit/widgetcenter/getcurrentconfigurations(_:)).
- An honest repair: "Forgot to log yesterday? Fill it in" opens yesterday's day sheet, and its close hides it for that day; no freezes or paid saves (https://udel.edu/udaily/2024/march/power-of-streaks-motivation-jackie-silverman, https://developer.apple.com/documentation/tipkit/tips/invalidationreason/tipclosed).

Trial promise and reminder (2026-10-21, SPEC 3.9, REDESIGN.md "Trial promise and reminder"). Tom: "No, don't send trial reminders, mate. Refunds get handled automatically through Apple. I don't know, actually. In terms of reminders, do deep research and just find what converts best for mobile apps, what sort of strategy." Tom first said no trial reminders; the research shows the paywall promise converts and the reminder is neutral, so both ship. The paywall shows "Today: full Pro", "Day 4: we remind you", "Day 7: $19.99 a year starts" under the yearly plan while the trial is on offer, all from StoreKit; right after a trial starts one short sheet asks for notifications for the evening nudge and that heads-up (one Continue button, then iOS's prompt, as Apple's HIG asks, https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/privacy), once nothing else holds the screen; and one reminder comes on day 4, at least 72 hours before the trial ends: "Your Taken trial ends Sunday 1 Nov. Unless cancelled, it then renews at $19.99 a year. 12 doses logged so far. Cancel at least 24 hours before in Settings > your name > Subscriptions." (Apple's steps, https://support.apple.com/en-us/118428), with its own switch in Profile, and on Today while notifications are off. Blinkist's promised reminder came with a "23% increase in conversion" (https://uxplanet.org/how-solving-our-biggest-customer-complaint-at-blinkist-led-to-a-23-increase-in-conversion-b60ad514134b), ShareFile's raised sign-ups 16.4% (https://marketingsherpa.com/article/case-study/simple-email-cancellation-reminder-lifts), and Utah asks for a notice at least 3 days before any trial ends (https://le.utah.gov/~2024/bills/hbillenr/HB0174.pdf). Also: Siri's "Did I take it?" asks a locked iPhone to unlock first (https://developer.apple.com/documentation/appintents/appintent/authenticationpolicy), and the age rating is 13+ (https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/reference/app-information/age-ratings-values-and-definitions/).

## Layout

| Path | What |
| --- | --- |
| `project.yml` | XcodeGen spec. The `.xcodeproj` is generated, never committed. |
| `Shared/` | Model, day logic, store, intents, reminders, the bundled name list and the type-ahead (`Catalog.swift`, `Suggest.swift`), the Taken Pro rule, cache and copy (`Pro.swift`, `ProCopy.swift`), and the looks (`Theme.swift`: every colour, radius and bracket a view or widget draws with, injected through the environment). Compiled into the app and the widget extension. |
| `Taken/` | The app: the tab bar and SwiftUI screens (`Views/`: Today, History, Profile, the sheets), Siri phrases, notification actions, fonts, assets. `Profile/` keeps the optional name and photo on the iPhone. |
| `Taken/Pro/` | StoreKit 2 (`ProStore`), the paywall with its trial timeline, and the one notification ask after a trial starts (`TrialAskSheet`). StoreKit runs only in the app; the widgets, control and intents read its cached answer, prices included. |
| `Taken/Taken.storekit` | Local StoreKit configuration (three products, the group, the introductory week), set on the scheme's Run action and never bundled. |
| `Taken/Todo/` | Voice to-dos: the To-do tab, on-device speech and the names it listens for (`TodoSpeech.swift`, `TodoVocabulary.swift`), the one list (`TodoRootView.swift`), the Listening card (`TodoListeningCard.swift`), long notes cut into parts and sorted one at a time (`TodoChunking.swift`, `NoteSorter.swift`), the Foundation Models sorter and the checks on what it returns (`TodoExtraction.swift`, `TodoFoundationModels.swift`, `TodoGrounding.swift`), the internal builds' sorting eval and voice note log (`TodoEval.swift`, `VoiceNoteLog.swift`), the Shortcuts actions for agents (`TodoAgentIntents.swift`), the Home Screen quick action. The data, store and the New voice to-do intent are `Shared/Todo*.swift`. |
| `TakenWidgets/` | Home and Lock Screen widgets, the Control Center controls (Take all, New voice to-do) and the Lock Screen to-do widget (its faces are in `Shared/TodoWidgetViews.swift`, so the app's widget gallery draws them too). |
| `TakenTests/` | Unit tests for day rollover and the wake-up time, frequencies, streaks, take all, undo, backfill, CSV, the store, the nudge plan, the Taken Pro entitlement rule, the gate, the paywall copy, voice to-dos (the one list, files from the list builds kept unchanged, the stop rule, every word kept, long notes in parts, sorting, grounding against the words said, due dates and clock times nobody said, Keep as one, notifications, the Shortcuts actions, the voice note log), the type-ahead and the catalog, Weekly and a weekly dose's week (`WeeklyWeekTests`), Today's one-time rows (`RetentionTests`), the trial timeline, the one ask and the trial reminder (`TrialReminderTests`, `ProCopyTests`), and the redesign (a frequency per added item, the profile, the tabs a capture opens, erase). |
| `scripts/` | CI helpers: Xcode selection, simulator choice (a 6.1 inch iPhone for the paywall's fit, `compact-simulator.sh`), screenshots. |

Data lives in one JSON file in the App Group `group.com.tombaxter.taken`, read and written through `NSFileCoordinator` so the app, widgets, intents and notification actions never overwrite each other.

## CI

`.github/workflows/ci.yml` runs on every push to `main` (docs and brand changes excluded) and on demand. On `macos-26` runners with the Xcode in `.xcode-version` (26.6), the same Xcode EAS ships with, it generates the project and, in parallel: builds and runs the unit tests on an iOS 26 iPhone simulator; builds Release for a generic iOS device without signing; and captures App Store screenshots on the iPhone 17 Pro Max simulator with `-demo` data. The PNGs are in the run's `screenshots` artifact.

The app accepts `-demo` (seeded stack, five weeks of history and a demo profile in temporary files, never the real ones) and `-screen today|today-done|add|settings|look|day|log|item` to open straight onto a state (`settings` and `look` open Profile, `log` the History tab, `day` a day sheet over it, `item` the item sheet), and `-theme signal|blush|phosphor|ice` to pick the look; CI captures today, today-done, History, the paywall, the widgets and Profile > Look in every look. `-screen widgets` renders the widget designs in the app for review and `-screen empty` shows first run. `-screen paywall` and `-screen locked` show Taken Pro's paywall and Today without Pro, `-screen settings-locked` Profile without Pro, `-screen paywall-no-yearly` the paywall when the App Store returned every plan but yearly, `-screen widgets-locked` the read-only widgets and `-screen widgets-standby` the small widget as StandBy and CarPlay draw it, on black without its canvas; under `-demo` StoreKit is never called (CI has no App Store account), so the paywall shows StoreKit-shaped demo plans. `-screen todo` shows the To-do tab with demo tasks on one list and two under Done today, `-screen todo-recording` shows it listening on the original card with the words on screen, `-screen todo-cap` a note past 4:00 ("1 minute left"), `-screen todo-empty` its first run, and `-screen widgets-todo` the Lock Screen to-do widget and the Control Center controls. `-screen add-search` opens the Add sheet in type mode with "tum" typed (Turmeric suggested); CI captures it and `todo` in every look. `-screen today-nudge-offer`, `today-repair` and `today-widget-invite` show Today's one-time rows (the evening nudge offer, the repair line for a partial yesterday, the widget invite under All taken), which `-demo` shows nowhere else, and `-screen widget-help` opens the sheet the invite leads to. The link checks fail the job unless `taken://pro` shows the paywall and `taken://todo` and `taken://todo/record` bring the To-do tab to the front over Profile, and the screen checks unless `-screen todo-cap` opens listening and `-screen add-search` opens the Add sheet (the demo app writes what is in front to `tmp/taken-ui-state.txt`).

CI cannot run Apple's on-device model, so internal TestFlight builds (the EAS `internal` profile, generated from `project.internal.yml` with the `TAKEN_INTERNAL` compilation condition and the `TakenInternalPro` marker) answer the link `taken://todo-eval`: open it from Safari on the iPhone and 15 fixed notes run through the real sorter (two of them 2 and 5-minute rambles, which pass only if every part sorts in 15 s or less), with the bar at 14 of 15. They also answer `taken://todo-log` and Profile > Internal > Voice note log: what each note heard, how it was cut and what was saved, kept on the iPhone and copied by hand. App Store builds do not contain the eval, the voice note log or the internal Pro unlock (App Review Guideline 2.5.19); `scripts/check-app-store-build.sh` checks the binary of every Release build in CI, release.yml and EAS.

To buy Taken Pro in the simulator, run from Xcode: the scheme's Run action uses `Taken/Taken.storekit`. A copy installed with `simctl` has no StoreKit configuration.

## Release to TestFlight

`.github/workflows/release.yml` runs on demand or on a `v*` tag. It first checks that a green ci.yml run on main covers the code (that commit or an ancestor, with nothing outside docs, brand and Markdown changed since), so nothing ships that the build, the tests and the screenshot link checks have not passed. It archives with manual signing, exports an App Store IPA and uploads it with the App Store Connect API key (`altool`, with `xcodebuild -exportArchive` upload as the fallback). The build number is the run number plus the optional `build_offset` input, so it always increases. A `v1.2.3` tag sets the version to 1.2.3.

Repository secrets (names only, values never go in the repo):

| Secret | Contents |
| --- | --- |
| `ASC_KEY_ID` | App Store Connect API key ID |
| `ASC_ISSUER_ID` | API key issuer ID |
| `ASC_KEY_P8_BASE64` | The `.p8` key, base64 |
| `DIST_CERT_P12_BASE64` | Apple Distribution certificate with private key, `.p12`, base64 |
| `DIST_CERT_PASSWORD` | Password of that `.p12` |
| `PROFILE_APP_BASE64` | App Store profile for `com.tombaxter.taken`, base64 |
| `PROFILE_WIDGETS_BASE64` | App Store profile for `com.tombaxter.taken.widgets`, base64 |

The same project also ships through EAS Build (`eas.json`; `.eas/build/build-ios.yml` for the App Store, `.eas/build/build-ios-internal.yml` for the `internal` profile), which runs `xcodegen generate` and signs with EAS-held credentials for the targets `Taken` and `TakenWidgets`. Internal builds are exported "TestFlight internal testing only", so App Store Connect keeps them for internal testers and never offers them for an App Store version or App Review; `scripts/check-eas-configs.sh` (CI) keeps the two configs' checks the same. `eas.json` names its build image in full (`macos-tahoe-26.5-xcode-26.6`), never an alias such as `latest`, which Expo moves to each new image without notice (it went from Xcode 26.6 to 27.1 on 2026-10-16). `scripts/check-xcode.sh` runs in CI, release.yml and EAS and fails the build unless the active Xcode is the one in `.xcode-version` and the `eas.json` image carries it, and it checks the built app's `DTXcode` and `DTXcodeBuild`, so a TestFlight or App Store build is built by the Xcode CI tested. A new Xcode means changing `.xcode-version`, the image and, if `macos-26` lacks that Xcode, the runner label, in one change. Release signing settings in `project.yml` are plain keys so either path can set them.

`.github/workflows/internal-testflight.yml` (on demand only) builds the internal TestFlight build without an EAS cloud build: after the "CI passed for this code" gate (a green ci.yml run on this commit or an ancestor, with nothing it checks changed since), it runs `eas build -p ios -e internal --local` on the `macos-26` runner with the internal profile's custom steps unchanged, then `eas submit` to TestFlight internal testing. eas-cli is pinned to 24.11.0. It needs the `EXPO_TOKEN` repository secret (an Expo robot token), set on the two eas steps only and never printed. It has shipped the internal builds since 1.1.0 (20) on 2026-10-20.

`.github/workflows/app-store-build.yml` (on demand, from main only) makes the App Store build the same way and with the same `EXPO_TOKEN`, with `eas build -p ios -e production --local`: generated from `project.yml`, with the production profile's custom steps unchanged. Before `eas submit` uploads it, `scripts/check-ipa.sh` unpacks the IPA and prints one OK or FAIL line per check, and nothing is uploaded unless all of them pass: the App Store shape (`check-app-store-build.sh`: no internal-only code, `TakenInternalPro` or `TFInternalTestingOnly`), iPhone only (`UIDeviceFamily` exactly `[1]` in the app and the widgets), the App Group in both signed entitlements, the version in `project.yml` with a build number above `FoundingMember.maxBuild`, the Xcode in `.xcode-version` (`check-xcode.sh`), and App Store distribution profiles. The IPA is never a workflow artifact, since anyone signed in to GitHub can download a public repository's artifacts. The 1.1 App Store build comes only from this workflow, or from an EAS cloud `production` build once the plan's iOS builds reset on 1 Nov; internal builds stay unsubmittable. App Store Connect also gives every upload to the internal group "Tom" (automatic distribution), so after an App Store build, run internal-testflight.yml once more to put Tom's iPhone back on the newest internal build.

Both workflows take their gate, Xcode, Node and fastlane from `.github/actions/ship-setup`, so they cannot drift apart, and they wait in one queue (`app-store-connect-upload`, `queue: max`): EAS's remote autoIncrement reads the last build number and then writes the next one, so two builds at once could take the same number. The gate ignores the two workflow files and that action, which ci.yml neither builds nor tests.

Optional repository variable `ASC_APP_ID`: the app's numeric Apple ID, passed to `altool` so the upload always lands on this app.

Both App IDs need the App Group capability with `group.com.tombaxter.taken`, and both profiles must include it. Expected profile names are `Taken App Store` and `Taken Widgets App Store`; the workflow reads the real names from the installed profiles, so other names also work. Team `F7YRP3B2U8`.
