The promise

Three promises. Nothing else.

Most pill apps open with a health quiz and an ad. Taken opens on the answer.

One tap

Tap it. It lights up.

Tap a pending item and it is logged with the minute you took it. Taking the whole stack at once? Take all is one tap too. A stray tap never un-logs anything.

Nothing to read

No funnel. No ads. No typing.

No onboarding screens, no account, no paywall nag. Add your stack from quick-pick chips in four taps. Typing a custom name is optional.

Nothing leaves your phone

Your log stays yours.

Everything lives on your iPhone. No servers, no analytics, no third-party code. Taken makes no network requests of its own; buying Taken Pro goes through Apple.

The record

A source of truth you can check at midnight.

Remember twice in one day? Open Taken and it tells you: taken at 07:42, nine hours ago. Five weeks of days sit underneath as a grid of lit ticks.

  • Exact timeEvery dose keeps the minute you logged it. TAKEN 07:42 / 9H AGO is the whole answer.
  • BackfillForgot to tap? Long press an item and set the real time, or tap any day in History to fix it.
  • Wake-up timeTell Taken when you wake up, and a 1 a.m. dose still counts for the day you were living.
  • ReminderOne optional nudge at a time you choose. It only fires if something is still pending, and its Take all button logs without opening the app.
  • SchedulesDaily, 2x or 3x daily with a pip per dose, alternate days, or the weekdays you pick.
  • ExportA CSV of every dose (date, time, item, code), only when you ask for it.

Where is my data stored?

Only on your iPhone, inside Taken and the container it shares with its own widgets. Nothing is uploaded, and there is no account to sign in to. The privacy policy has the detail.

I tapped the wrong item.

Tap Undo on the toast that appears for four seconds. Later on, long press the item and choose Undo last dose.

I took it but forgot to log it.

Long press the item and choose Set time to log it at the real time. For an earlier day, tap that day in History and mark it there.

How do I add the widget or the control?

Home Screen: touch and hold an empty spot, tap Edit, then Add Widget, and choose Taken. Lock Screen: touch and hold the Lock Screen, tap Customize, then the widget area. Control Center (iOS 18 and later): open Control Center, tap the plus, then Add a Control, and choose Take all.

What can I say to Siri?

Try Did I take my pills in Taken, Check my stack in Taken, Log my stack in Taken or I took my pills in Taken. The same actions are in the Shortcuts app.

Does it work without a connection?

Always. Logging, your history and the widgets need no connection. Only buying or restoring Taken Pro talks to Apple's App Store.

How do I delete my data?

In Taken, tap your avatar at the top right to open your profile, and choose Erase all data. Deleting the app also deletes everything it stored.

Is this medical advice?

No. Taken is a personal log. It does not recommend doses or replace your doctor or pharmacist. Follow the instructions you were given.
