Privacy Policy

Effective 13 October 2026 Publisher: Thomas Baxter Applies to Taken for iPhone

The short versionTaken collects no data. Everything you log stays on your iPhone, and a voice note is turned into to-dos on the iPhone itself. We never see it, store it, sell it or share it, because it never reaches us.

01Who we are

Taken is a pill and supplement log for iPhone, published by Thomas Baxter (we, us). This policy covers the Taken app, its widgets, its Control Center controls, its Siri and Shortcuts actions, and this website.

02Data we collect

None. Taken does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data, health data, usage data or device identifiers. We do not operate any server that receives information from the app.

03What the app stores on your iPhone

To do its job, Taken keeps the following on your device:

  • the items in your stack: names, short code tags, schedules and order;
  • the date and time of each dose you log;
  • your settings: reminder on or off and its time, when your day starts, haptics, and the look you chose;
  • your to-dos: the words of each one, when it is due if you said, and when you finished it (never the audio of a voice note);
  • a small Taken Pro status file: whether Pro is active as Apple's App Store last reported it (the plan, and when it renews or ends), when you first logged a dose, and whether the Taken Pro offer has been shown.

This information is stored only on your iPhone, in the app's private storage and in an App Group container that Taken shares with its own widgets and Siri actions so they can show your progress and log doses. It is never sent to us or to anyone else.

04Voice to-dos

When you tap the mic in the To-do tab, Taken uses the microphone and Apple's on-device speech recognition to turn what you say into text, then sorts that text into to-dos with Apple's on-device model (Apple Intelligence, on iPhones that have it). Speech and task sorting happen on your iPhone. The audio is never stored and never sent anywhere, and neither are the words; only the to-dos you see are kept, on your iPhone, as listed above.

Taken asks for microphone and speech recognition permission the first time you tap the mic, and listens only while the mic is on. You can turn either permission off at any time in iOS Settings, and type your to-dos instead. iOS may download Apple's speech model for your language once; that download comes from Apple and carries nothing you said.

05What we do not do

  • No accounts and no sign-in.
  • No analytics, no crash reporting services and no usage tracking.
  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers.
  • No third-party SDKs or libraries of any kind.
  • No network requests of our own. Taken works offline; to buy or check Taken Pro, iOS contacts Apple's App Store.
  • No tracking across other companies' apps or websites.

06Purchases

Taken Pro is bought through Apple's App Store with your Apple Account. Apple processes the payment under Apple's own privacy policy; we never see your payment details or your Apple Account.

To unlock Pro, Taken asks Apple's StoreKit on your iPhone whether there is an active Taken Pro purchase on your Apple Account, and which version of Taken you first downloaded (everyone whose first download was Taken 1.0 keeps Pro for life). iOS gets that answer from the App Store. Taken keeps only the result on your iPhone, in the status file listed above, so its widgets, Control Center control and Siri actions work without asking again. Nothing about your purchases reaches us, and they are never linked to your log.

07Notifications

If you turn on the reminder, Taken schedules local notifications on your iPhone. They are created and delivered by iOS on your device. No push service and no server is involved. Taken asks for notification permission only when you switch the reminder on, and you can turn it off at any time in Taken or in iOS Settings. Once notifications are allowed, a to-do with a time also gets a local notification at that time.

08Siri, Shortcuts and widgets

Widgets, the Control Center control and Siri or Shortcuts actions read and update the same on-device log. When you speak to Siri, Apple processes your request under Apple's own privacy policy. Taken only receives the action you asked for, such as logging your stack, and answers from the log stored on your iPhone. New voice to-do, from Siri, Shortcuts, the Action button, Control Center, the Lock Screen or the app icon, only opens Taken; listening starts on screen, in the app.

09CSV export

Taken creates a CSV file of your log only when you choose Export CSV. You decide where it goes using the iOS share sheet. Once you share the file, the app or person you share it with handles it under their own terms.

10Backups

If you use iCloud Backup or back up your iPhone to a computer, iOS may include Taken's data in that backup, as it does for most apps. Backups are handled by Apple and by you. We have no access to them.

11Deleting your data

You are always in control. In Taken, open Settings and choose Erase all data to delete your stack, your log, your to-dos and your settings; the look you chose and the Taken Pro status file described above stay. Deleting the app from your iPhone deletes everything it stored on the device, including the shared container used by its widgets. A Taken Pro purchase belongs to your Apple Account, and Restore brings it back.

12Children

Taken is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children under 13 or the minimum age that applies in your country.

13Your rights

Privacy laws such as the UK GDPR, the EU GDPR and the California Consumer Privacy Act give you rights over personal data a company holds about you. Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct, export or delete on our side. Your log is on your device, under your control.

14This website

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. It sets no cookies and uses no analytics, trackers or third-party scripts, and its fonts are served from the site itself. GitHub, as the host, may process technical data such as IP addresses to deliver and secure the site, under the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

15Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above. If a change is significant, we will also say so in the app's release notes.

16Contact

Questions about this policy: email tjpb2@cantab.ac.uk. You can also open an issue at github.com/tjbaxter/taken/issues; issues are public, so please do not include health details or other personal information there.
