02What Taken is

Taken is a personal log for the pills and supplements you take, with to-dos and an optional weight log. Taken is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any medical condition. It does not tell you what to take, how much or when. Always consult a healthcare professional for medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment, and follow the instructions you were given for your medicines.

Taken shows what you logged, nothing more. A log can be wrong (a missed tap, a wrong time, a phone's clock), and a reminder can be late or not arrive (battery saving, notification settings or Do Not Disturb). Do not rely on Taken alone where a missed or extra dose could harm you.
