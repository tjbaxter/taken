A calmer Taken. Today, To-do and History each have their own tab. A + beside your stack adds to it, and each item gets its own schedule as you add it. Your profile holds a name, a photo and every setting, and a wake-up time decides which day a late dose counts for.

Voice to-dos: tap the mic and say what you need to do. With Apple Intelligence, Taken sorts it into a list on your iPhone; without it, the note is saved as said. Keep as one puts a note back together with a tap. Every note goes onto one list, with nothing to choose.

Adding is quicker: type the first letters of a name and pick it from the suggestions, spelled right for you. A Weekly schedule puts a once-a-week dose on its day, and a dose logged in the days after it still counts for its week. There are three more quick-pick chips.

Today offers the evening nudge once, helps you fill in yesterday if you forgot to log, and shows you how to add the Lock Screen widget the first day everything is taken.

Shortcuts: Did I take it? now hands its answer on (on a locked iPhone it asks you to unlock first), and Get to-dos and Add to-do let a shortcut you build read and add to your list. Taken itself sends nothing.

Taken Pro is here. Log your first dose, then pick yearly (7 days on us for new subscribers, with a reminder 3 days before the trial ends), monthly or lifetime. Everyone who downloaded Taken 1.0 keeps Pro for life, at no cost. Still no ads, no account and no tracking, and your log stays on your iPhone.

Four looks in your profile: Signal, Blush, Phosphor and Ice. Your widgets and the app icon follow the one you pick.

New: an optional Weight tab. Log a weigh-in in a few taps and watch a smoothed trend form. Turn it on in Profile.
