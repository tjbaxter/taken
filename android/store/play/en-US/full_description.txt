It's late, and you can't remember: did I take it today? Taken is the source of truth for your daily pills and supplements. Tap a row when you take one, and look later to know, down to the minute.

One tap
• Tap a row on Today and it's logged, with the time beside it.
• Take all logs every item still pending at once.
• A second tap never un-logs a dose, and Undo is right there.
• To change an item, tap the pencil. Touch and hold a row for Undo last dose and Set time.

The answer, any time
• Not sure an hour later? The answer is on screen the moment Taken opens.
• Forgot to log? Set the real time, or fill in an earlier day in History.
• Set your wake-up time, and a late-night dose still counts for the day you were living.

Every schedule
• Daily, every other day, weekly, twice or three times a day, or the days you pick, set for each item as you add it.
• A weekly shot sits on its day. Logged a few days late, it still counts for its week.
• Items not due today wait under Not today, with the day they are next due.
• Add your stack from quick-pick chips, or type the first letters and pick a suggestion.

Log it without opening Taken
• Home screen widget: tap a row to log it, or tap Take all.
• Quick Settings tile: swipe down and tap Take all, even before you unlock.

A reminder that stays quiet
• One optional evening nudge, around the time you choose.
• It only appears if something is still pending. All done? Silence.
• Take all straight from the notification.

Your record
• History shows five weeks of days at a glance, with your streak and 30-day completion.
• Export every dose to CSV whenever you want it.

Four looks
• Signal, Blush, Phosphor or Ice. Pick one in Profile and your widgets follow.

Voice to-dos
• Tap the mic and say what you need to do. Taken saves it as you said it, one line per sentence, on one list. Nothing to choose.
• Keep as one puts a note back together. Tap the circle when a to-do is done.
• Speech becomes text on your phone, never sent anywhere, and the audio is never saved. Voice needs on-device speech recognition (Android 12 or later); you can always type instead.

Weight, if you want it
• An optional Weight tab, off until you turn it on in Profile.
• Type a weigh-in and watch a smoothed trend form. Weigh-ins follow the same Taken Pro rule as doses.

Your log stays yours
• No account, no ads, no tracking, no analytics. Taken has no servers and never sends your log anywhere.
• Your log, to-dos and settings stay on your phone, and in Android's own end-to-end encrypted backup if you use it.
• Purchases go through Google Play.
