## 1. The job

"It's the end of the day and I can't remember: did I take it today?"

Taken is a source of truth for a daily pill / supplement stack. Tap when taken. Look later and know for sure, with the time. Nothing else gets in the way.

Founder's words (verbatim, the bar): "Fucking frictionless. No ads, no text, no typing, literally tap, tap, tap, done." "All the apps I've seen are so complicated. You have to go through like a 20 step health funnel. It's interrupted by ads." "If I remember twice in a day, I can be like, oh, I actually did take them nine hours ago."

His stack (the first user): Vitamin D3 (daily), Finasteride (daily or every other day), Fish Oil (daily, 1 or 2 caps).

Non-negotiables:
- Zero onboarding funnel, zero accounts, zero ads, zero analytics/tracking, zero network calls of our own. Everything stays on the phone. Taken Pro (1.1) is bought through Apple's StoreKit, which talks to the App Store itself (3.9, PRO.md).
- Logging a dose = ONE tap from the app, the widget, the Lock Screen, Control Center, a notification, or Siri.
- Adding the stack needs no typing (quick-pick chips). Typing a custom name is optional.
- Opening the app answers the question in under one second: each item shows the exact time it was taken beside a filled ring, or an empty ring while pending; how long ago is one tap on the ring.
- Tapping something already taken never un-takes it by accident.

## 3. Product

### 3.1 Tabs, Today and History (1.1, REDESIGN.md)
Three tabs in the system tab bar (`TabView`, Liquid Glass on iOS 26): **Today** (the stack), **To-do** (voice to-dos, 3.10) and **History** (the calendar and stats). A real launch opens on Today. The round avatar at the top right of every tab (the profile photo, else the initials, else a person glyph; 44 pt) opens Profile (3.4); there is no gear. Every sheet (Add, Profile, the item sheet, the day sheet, Set time, a to-do's edit and typing sheets) and the Taken Pro paywall are presented from the root above the tabs (`RootView`, `SheetRoute`, `.paywallHost(.root)` on the tab view), so a voice to-do request closes whatever is open and brings the To-do tab to the front (`AppModel.openTodoTab`, through `.opensTodoTab`).

Today: a large title "Today" in Geist with the date under it ("Tuesday 13 October"), then one calm progress line in doses, as the widgets count them: "4 of 6 taken" (the numbers in Geist Mono, "Last 07:44" on the right) over a slim bar. When everything due is taken the line becomes the All taken moment: a check in the look's signal springs in with the success haptic, "All taken" in the readable signal, the last dose's time and the streak. No counter panel, ruler, brackets, scanline or status labels.

Stack: the title "Stack" with a clear "+" beside it (44 pt, the look's signal ink, VoiceOver "Add to stack") that opens Add, then a card with one row per item due today, in the user's order. Each row:
- the name (Geist) over a small frequency line ("Daily", "2x daily · 1 of 2", "Every other day").
- on the right, the ring that logs: empty while pending, one arc per dose for a 2x or 3x item, and once everything due is taken the look's signal with a check and the time beside it ("07:42", Geist Mono). In locked mode a small lock sits in a pending ring.
- Tap the ring of a pending item: mark taken now. Light impact haptic, the ring fills, a toast "Vitamin D3 taken at 07:42 · Undo" for 4 s (undo removes that dose).
- Tap the ring of a taken item: never untakes. It shows the detail ("Taken at 07:42 · 9h 12m ago") with a soft haptic.
- Tap the name: the item sheet (3.4). Long-press the row: Undo last dose, Set time… (correct when it was really taken, or log it at that time), Edit item. VoiceOver: the row is one element whose default action is the ring; Edit item, Undo last dose and Set time are its actions.
- Multi-dose items (2x/3x daily): each tap on the ring fills the next arc with its own timestamp; done when all are filled.
- Items not due today (an every-other-day item taken yesterday) sit in a quiet "Not today" group under the stack, with "Every other day · Next: tomorrow" ("Next: Thursday", "Next: 21 Oct"). Its ring still logs a dose (no lockout).

Locked mode (no Taken Pro after the first dose, 3.9): everything above stays on screen and readable. A slim bar under the progress line reads "Start 7 days on us" (while the introductory offer applies) or "Pro ended. Tap to continue", and a tap opens the paywall. Logging a dose, Take all (it carries a small lock), Set time, backfill, adding items and turning the nudge on open the paywall instead, and the tap that opened it is applied once Pro is on. Undo, removing a dose, the item sheet, the day sheet and CSV export always work.

Take all: when 2+ items are pending, a plain full-width "Take all" button with the count in Geist Mono sits above the tab bar, with the undo toast. One tap marks every pending due dose taken at the same time. With fewer it is hidden. Repeat guard: Take all (here and from a widget, the control, Siri or the nudge) and a widget row skip an item logged in the last hour, so a second tap never logs a 2x or 3x item's next dose on top of the first; the count on the button is what it will log. A tap on the ring in the app is never held back.

History: a large title "History" ("The last five weeks"), the streak ("14 days": consecutive complete days, today counts once complete, a day with nothing due does not break it) and the last 30 days ("97%": due doses taken / due doses) as two cards, then a 5-week grid (Mon-first, today at bottom right, outlined): a lit check where everything due was taken, a half-lit bar for some, a dim dot for none taken or none due, nothing for days to come. Tap a day: the day sheet, listing that day's items with their taken times; each item can be marked taken at a time (an hour after the wake-up time on an earlier day, now for today, adjustable) or a dose removed. This is the backfill path for "I forgot to log yesterday".

### 3.2 Empty state / first run
No onboarding screens and nothing asked (no profile, no look). First launch shows Today with "Nothing in your stack yet", "Tap what you take. It takes a few seconds." and an "Add to stack" button that opens the Add sheet.

### 3.3 Add sheet (no typing needed)
A grid of quick-pick chips, exactly as 1.0 had them (multi-select, tap to toggle, mono caps): Vitamin D3, Fish Oil, Omega-3, Magnesium, Creatine, Multivitamin, Zinc, Vitamin C, Vitamin B12, Iron, Probiotic, Ashwagandha, Melatonin, Collagen, Finasteride, Minoxidil, Birth Control, Metformin, Semaglutide, Tirzepatide, Electrolytes, Vitamin K2, Folate, Calcium, Turmeric, CoQ10, L-Theanine, 27 in all (Birth Control `BCP`, Semaglutide `SEM` and Tirzepatide `TRZ` joined in the 1.1 feedback build; generic names only). Each chip carries a sensible default code tag (3 characters, unique). Plus a `+ CUSTOM` chip that opens type mode (the only place typing exists, and always optional). Target: Tom adds his whole stack in 4 taps.
- Type mode (`Taken/Views/AddSheet.swift`): `+ CUSTOM` shows its selected state and a 50 pt field ("Search or type a name", today's raised background and focus edge, a clear button while it has text, words capitalisation, no autocorrection, Done) slides in pinned under the sheet header, above the chips' ScrollView (opacity combined with a move from the top, `.snappy(duration: 0.28)`), so neither the keyboard nor the Add bar can cover it. Focus is set from the field's own `onAppear` after one main-actor hop (`defaultFocus` does not fire for a field inserted by a tap). There is no matchedGeometryEffect across the ScrollView edge (unverified there). While the field has focus the Add bar steps away and comes back when the keyboard goes; nothing animates per keystroke. A pick (a suggestion, or Return) plays the selection haptic and closes the field back into `+ CUSTOM`: a chip's name lights that chip, any other name becomes a selected chip just before `+ CUSTOM` with its How often row. Losing focus with text keeps the text as a pick, as before, and brings the Add bar back; losing focus with the field empty leaves type mode.
- Type-ahead (`Shared/Suggest.swift`, `Suggest.rank`, pure, unit tested): up to 4 matches under the field, then `+ Add "<typed text>"` last in the readable signal (left out only when the text is exactly a catalog name). 48 pt rows in a card, the typed letters muted and the rest semibold, a brand alias faint after its generic name ("Tirzepatide · Mounjaro"), an item already in the stack faint with a check and not pickable, nothing while the field is empty. Rows are capped to the height measured under the field, so on an iPhone SE with the keyboard up (about four rows) or at large text the field stays in view and nothing scrolls. VoiceOver hears "N suggestions" once typing pauses (0.4 s). Return picks the first row that can be picked and never rewrites what was typed. Matching folds case, accents and width, drops apostrophes and also compares a form without spaces (coq10 finds CoQ10, omega3 finds Omega-3). Tiers: exact, starts with, every typed word starts a word of the name (vit d, lions mane), then typos (optimal string alignment distance to a prefix of the name: none up to 3 letters, 1 from 4, 2 from 8; a first-letter typo ranks as two, Algolia's defaults). Common misspellings (tumeric, ashwaganda, magnisium, glucosomine) are hidden aliases that rank as one typo. Ties: tier, typos, chips first, the catalog's weight, a name before an alias, the shorter name, then alphabetical. No phonetics, no model.
- The bundled list (`Shared/Catalog.swift`, `Catalog.entries`, 299 names): ingredient-level vitamins and minerals with the forms people buy, botanicals, other supplements, common daily medicines by generic name (US names as RxNorm spells them, the UK name as an alias), the two GLP-1s (and Oral Semaglutide, the once-daily tablet: Rybelsus and the Wegovy pill are its aliases) and Birth Control. Spellings and brand-to-generic mappings were checked offline against RxNorm and the NIH DSLD; the app never looks a name up: there is no runtime lookup of any kind and the list ships in the binary. Brand names are aliases only, shown faint in suggestions, never as chip labels, in metadata or in screenshots. No retatrutide, CagriSema or research peptides (BPC-157, TB-500, GHK-Cu, CJC-1295, ipamorelin, MOTS-c, Semax, Epitalon, sermorelin) as entries or aliases (unit tested); anything can still be typed and added through the `+ Add` row. `Catalog.presets` (the chips) is derived from the entries, and `Catalog.code(for:)` reads names, then aliases and misspellings ("Mounjaro" is TRZ), then derives a code as before (Lion's Mane is LMN).
- How often: once something is picked, each pick has its own menu: Daily (the default), Every other day, Weekly, 2x daily, 3x daily, Days (weekday toggles under it; Days with none picked adds it as daily), so Finasteride can be every other day while D3 is daily, in one go (`AddPlan.drafts(selected:extras:choices:days:weekly:weekday:)`, unit tested: a pick that is not a chip has its own frequency and takes its catalog code when it has one, and a name picked twice is added once). "Add 3" confirms.
- Weekly: saved as a one-day Days set (`.weekdays([d])`), so the file format does not change, and a one-day set reads back as Weekly. A single-select weekday row under it starts on the weekday of the day being lived; the item sheet offers the same. It reads "Weekly · Thu", and "Weekly · Next: Thursday" under Not today. Semaglutide and Tirzepatide (and the brand names of their injections typed in full: Ozempic, Wegovy, Mounjaro, Zepbound) start on Weekly; Oral Semaglutide (Rybelsus, the Wegovy pill, typed or picked) starts on Daily, since the tablet is taken once a day; Semaglutide picked from its chip for a tablet is one switch to Daily; Birth Control starts on Daily. Missed-day, streak and nudge logic is unchanged: the nudge fires only on days with something pending, so a weekly item is nudged on its day, and a late weekly dose logs from its ring under Not today.

### 3.4 Profile (from the avatar) and the item sheet
Profile opens from the avatar on any tab. Nothing in it is asked for; it is all optional.
- Header: the avatar (92 pt) with a camera badge opens Apple's photo picker (`.photosPicker`, out of process, no photo library permission); the photo is kept as its centre square, at most 600 px, JPEG (`profile-photo.jpg`), shown in a circle; "Remove photo" under it. The name field ("Your name", `profile.json`); the initials stand in for a photo. Both live in the App Group folder next to the log and only the app reads them (`ProfileStore`). A summary line: "14-day streak · 97% over 30 days", or "Your streak starts with your first dose."
- Stack: list of items (name, frequency), reorder (Reorder in the header), swipe to delete (with confirm), tap for the item sheet, "Add to stack". Archived items follow, dimmed.
- Look: the four looks of section 4 as live swatches, one tap each; the chosen one is ringed in the current look's signal and the header names it. The app recolours at once, the widgets and the control follow, and the app icon follows when Profile closes. Footer: "The widgets and the app icon follow it."
- Reminder: one global nudge. Toggle + time, "If anything is still pending at this time, Taken nudges you." Until a time is chosen the nudge is 13 hours after the wake-up time (20:00 for 07:00). Ask notification permission only when the user turns this on. In locked mode a line reads "The nudge needs Taken Pro" (a tap opens the paywall), turning Nudge on opens the paywall and comes on with the permission prompt once Pro is on, and turning it off always works.
- Your day, **Wake-up time** (Tom 2026-10-13: "this is confusing, fix this ... don't say don't"): default 07:00, any time of day, a Geist Mono time picker, one line: "Doses count for the day you wake up into." Code owns the day boundary: the day rolls over at the wake-up time minus 4 hours, to the hour, clamped to 00:00 through 12:00 (wake 08:00: 04:00; 05:00: 01:00; 14:00: 10:00; 02:00: 00:00; `StackSettings.dayStartHour`, unit tested). Nobody sees or sets the rollover. A file with a `dayStartHour` and no wake-up time (1.0) reads as wake-up = day start + 4 hours, so nobody's history moves; both keys are written, so an earlier build still rolls over at the same hour. The wake-up time also sets the day sheet's backfill time on an earlier day (wake-up + 1 hour) and the nudge until one is chosen (wake-up + 13 hours).
- Feel: Haptics toggle (default on).
- Taken Pro (after Feel, before Data): the status line ("Founding member · Pro for life", "Pro yearly · renews 12 Oct 2027", "Pro yearly · ends ..." once cancelled, "Pro lifetime", "Trial · ends 19 Oct", "Pro monthly · billing issue" in a grace period, "Pro yearly · renewing" while a renewal is on its way, "Not active"), "See plans" when not active, "Restore purchases" (nothing found: "No active Taken Pro on this Apple Account."), "Redeem code" (offer code sheet), "Manage subscription" (Apple's sheet; for lifetime and founding members only while a subscription on the account still renews, under "Your subscription still renews. Taken Pro is yours for good, so you can cancel it."), "Terms of Use" (Apple's standard EULA) and "Privacy Policy".
- Data: Export CSV (share sheet; columns date, time, item, code), Erase all data (confirm: "This removes your stack, every logged dose, all your to-dos and your profile from this iPhone. It cannot be undone."). It also deletes any unreadable copy a write set aside (`taken-unreadable-*.json`, `todos-unreadable-*.json`); the look, the Taken Pro status file and the date of the first voice note stay.
- About: "Everything stays on this iPhone. No accounts. No ads. No tracking.", version + build, Support link (https://tjbaxter.github.io/taken/).

The item sheet (a tap on an item's name on Today, or its row in Profile): Name, How often (the same menu as Add, weekday toggles for Days, one weekday for Weekly), Archive ("Hidden from Today. History stays."), Delete item (with confirm). A new frequency applies from today and earlier days keep the one they had; unarchiving keeps the archived days out of history (a dose logged for one of them still counts). Deleting an item keeps its past log entries out of stats but never crashes history. A new name takes its preset's code tag, or one derived from it (the widgets and the nudge show codes).

### 3.5 Reminders (local notifications only)
- If enabled: at the nudge time, if any due dose for the current day is still pending, a notification `2 pending: VD3, FSH` with action buttons `TAKE ALL` (runs in background, no app open, marks all pending due doses taken now) and `OPEN`.
- Implementation must not nag when everything is done: schedule individual (non-repeating) requests for the next 14 days; cancel/reschedule today's whenever state changes (dose logged in app, widget, intent, notification action) and on every app foreground. Content lists what is pending as of scheduling, refreshed on every state change.
- Nudges follow Taken Pro (3.9): none are scheduled without it, and a subscription set not to renew is nudged only until it ends. A nudge's TAKE ALL while locked logs nothing, says `Logging needs Taken Pro. Open Taken to continue.`, and Taken offers that TAKE ALL with the paywall when it next opens.
- Each nudge carries its logical day. Its TAKE ALL logs only on that day: last night's nudge tapped this morning logs nothing and says so. Tapping a nudge opens the app on that day. Delivered nudges for earlier days, and today's once nothing is pending, are withdrawn on every reschedule.

### 3.6 Widgets (TakenWidgets extension, interactive via App Intents, iOS 17+)
- Home Screen small: big `2/3` + mini pips, and a single tappable `TAKE ALL` (or `ALL TAKEN` locked state).
- Home Screen medium: up to 4 item rows, each a Button(intent:) that logs that item's next dose; header counter.
- Lock Screen: accessoryCircular (gauge 2/3, or a check when complete), accessoryRectangular (up to 3 items with ●/○ and times), accessoryInline (`2/3 TAKEN` / `ALL TAKEN 07:42`). Make the circular/rectangular ones log `TAKE ALL` on tap via intent where the system allows; otherwise they deep-link into the app.
- Control Center (iOS 18+, `@available`): a ControlWidgetButton `Take all` with the app's glyph.
- Every write from a widget/intent calls `WidgetCenter.shared.reloadAllTimelines()` (and ControlCenter reload) and reschedules reminders. Timelines refresh at the next day rollover.
- Without Taken Pro (3.9) every widget shows the stack read-only with a small `PRO` lock glyph, its buttons give way to links, and any tap opens `taken://pro` (the paywall). A medium-widget row opens `taken://pro?then=log&item=<id>`, and that dose is logged once Pro is on. The timeline adds an entry at the moment cached Pro ends (`ProStatus.activeUntil`, which for a plan set to renew is 3 days past its period end, so a renewal never locks a widget). The Control Center control reads the same cache (a ControlValueProvider): locked, it shows `Taken Pro` with a lock and logs nothing; the tap is kept and Taken offers it when opened (controls can only open an app through universal links, which Taken does not have).
- Widget visual style matches the app in the chosen look (its canvas, mono type, signal colour and radii), read from the App Group store with the stack (`WidgetSnapshot.theme`) and set as the environment's theme; `containerBackground(for: .widget)` is the look's canvas. Every family works in every look, including the iOS 18+ tinted and clear rendering modes (widgetAccentable on the signal elements; outside full colour, TAKE ALL is an outline and unlit segments stay faint, since every opaque fill renders white). Lock Screen widgets are drawn by the system in white and look the same in every look. Where the system removes the canvas and draws the widget on black (StandBy, CarPlay: `showsWidgetContainerBackground` is false), a light look switches to `Theme.withoutBackground`: its signal, shapes and brackets on Signal's dark-canvas text roles (unit tested for AA on black; `-screen widgets-standby` shows it). The Control Center control takes the look's signal colour as its tint (its value provider reads the look with the Pro cache).

### 3.7 Siri / Shortcuts (App Intents, AppShortcutsProvider)
- `Take all` intent: phrases "Log my stack in ${applicationName}", "I took my pills in ${applicationName}". Result dialog: "Logged 3: Vitamin D3, Fish Oil, Finasteride."
- `Did I take it?` intent: phrases "Did I take my pills in ${applicationName}", "Check my stack in ${applicationName}". Dialog answers from the log: "Yes. Vitamin D3 at 7:42, Fish Oil at 7:42. Finasteride is not due today." or "Not yet: Fish Oil is pending." It also returns that sentence as its value (`ReturnsValue<String>`, exactly the words Siri says), so a shortcut can hand it to another app or a model.
- `Log item` intent with an AppEntity parameter for one item.
- Without Taken Pro the logging intents write nothing and answer "Logging needs Taken Pro. Open Taken to continue." The request is kept for 10 minutes in the App Group, and when Taken opens it offers it with the paywall (logged at the time it was asked for once Pro is on). `Did I take it?` always answers.
- The agent read surface (1.1 feedback build, `Taken/Todo/TodoAgentIntents.swift`, app target only): Shortcuts actions that hand values on, the way Things and Streaks do, so "Ask Claude", "Ask ChatGPT" or Apple's "Use Model" can work with them in a shortcut the person builds. Everything runs on the iPhone; there is no server, no key and no account, and Taken itself sends nothing.
  - `TodoEntity` (AppEntity): the task's id, and title, due (a time as said, or a day alone as the end of that day), is done, done at and list (Personal or Work), shown as the title with its due label. `TodoEntityQuery` is an `EnumerableEntityQuery` over the to-do store.
  - `Get to-dos`: "Your to-dos from Taken, for use in other shortcuts." Show: Open to-dos (default), Done today or All to-dos; List: Personal, Work, or both when empty. Returns `ReturnsValue<[TodoEntity]>` in the To-do tab's order (with no list, open ones come the list on top first, each list due first; All is those, then done ones newest first; Done today stays newest first across both), with a dialog giving the count and up to 3 titles ("3 open to-dos: Call the dentist, Send the deck and Buy milk."). Needs no Taken Pro. App Shortcut phrase "What's on my to-do list in ${applicationName}" (5 App Shortcuts of the 10 allowed). `authenticationPolicy` is `.requiresAuthentication`: Siri would otherwise read both lists (and up to 30 days of done ones) to anyone holding a locked iPhone, so a locked iPhone asks for Face ID or the passcode first. The Lock Screen widget shows the first two open titles of both lists, in the same order (3.10).
  - `Add to-do`: title, optional due (saved as a time), optional list (the list on top of the To-do tab when empty, where voice notes go; never the old list in view). Its List parameter reads "Personal or Work. Leave it empty for the list on top in Taken." and its description "Adds a to-do to Taken, on the list you choose or the one on top." Gated exactly like a typed note (`TodoGate` over the cached Pro status, with `ProRecheck` for a lapsed renewing plan; the first note is still on the house). Locked, it writes nothing and stops with "To-dos need Taken Pro. Open Taken to continue." An empty title asks again. Added, it writes through `TodoStore.update`, reloads the to-do widget, reschedules the due-time notifications and returns the new `TodoEntity`. It has no phrase, since a phrase cannot carry a title. Like `Get to-dos` it requires authentication on a locked iPhone (a write, and its answer reads the to-do back).
  - Not built: no "Connect your agent" screen, no in-app send to an AI service (that would make Taken the sharer under App Review 5.1.2(i)), no Spotlight indexing of the stack or doses.

### 3.8 Data and correctness (code owns truth)
- Model: `Item { id: UUID, name, code, frequency (.daily(times: 1...3) | .alternateDays | .weekdays(Set<Int>), Weekly being a one-day set), createdAt, archived, archivedAt, sortIndex, pastFrequencies: [{ until, frequency }]?, pauses: [{ from, until }]? }`, `Dose { id, itemId, takenAt: Date, utcOffset: Int? }`, `StackSettings { wakeMinutes, reminderEnabled, reminderMinutes?, hapticsEnabled, theme }` with `dayStartHour` derived from the wake-up time (3.4). Day membership of a dose = calendar day of (takenAt - dayStartHour) at the UTC offset where it was logged (`utcOffset`; doses saved before it existed use the current zone). Each day uses the frequency in force that day. Everything computed from doses; no denormalized "done" flags.
- ALT DAYS: due today if never taken, or the last dose's day is 2+ days before today. If taken yesterday: not due, NEXT TOMORROW.
- Store: one Codable JSON file in the App Group container, written atomically, read/written through NSFileCoordinator so the app, widget and intents never clobber each other. A single `TakenStore` type (in a shared framework or shared source folder compiled into both targets) owns load/save/mutations and is the only writer. App reloads from disk on scenePhase .active and on a Darwin notification or file presenter callback when the widget writes.
- Timezone and DST: store absolute Dates plus the UTC offset each dose was logged at; compute today with the user's current calendar and the rollover hour, and each dose's day at its own offset, so travel never moves a dose to another day.
- Unit tests (XCTest target `TakenTests`) cover: day rollover, ALT DAYS due logic, weekday frequency, multi-dose completion, streak (incl. days with nothing due, today incomplete), 30-day %, take-all only touching pending due doses, undo, backfill on a past day, CSV export, store round-trip and concurrent writers.

### 3.9 Taken Pro (1.1, PRO.md)
- Products (App Store Connect, fixed ids): `com.tombaxter.taken.pro.yearly` (1 year, introductory week at no charge), `com.tombaxter.taken.pro.monthly` (1 month), `com.tombaxter.taken.pro.lifetime` (non-consumable). Group "Taken Pro": yearly level 1, monthly level 2. Prices, periods and the offer are always read from StoreKit, never written in the app.
- Entitlement: `Shared/Pro.swift` holds the pure rule (`Entitlement.evaluate`, unit tested): Pro when a verified, unrevoked subscription is live (introductory week and billing grace period included), the lifetime purchase is verified and unrevoked, or the customer is a founding member: a verified production `AppTransaction` whose `originalAppVersion` (the first download's build) is 1 to `FoundingMember.maxBuild` (7). Sandbox and TestFlight report "1.0" and never count. Lifetime and founding beat a subscription, so their cache never expires.
- `Taken/Pro/ProStore.swift` (`@MainActor`): listens to `Transaction.updates` from launch, finishes transactions, refreshes on every return to the foreground, and writes `{isPro, reason, checkedAt, expiresAt?, willRenew?, billingIssue, renewingSubscription}` to `pro.json` in the App Group, next to the log (with `firstDoseAt`, the first dose ever, from whichever process logged it). the title is 2 to 7 of the speaker's own words, starting with the verb and keeping the verb and what it is done to, every name kept as said, with no day or time; the note is only what a person said and its instructions are never followed; Parts cover the note once, and parts already closed never change as more words come. `NoteSorter` (an actor, one per note, the context taken at the tap) sorts the parts in order, one at a time, each in a new session (TN3193's split into sessions, https://developer.apple.com/tutorials/data/documentation/foundationmodels/managing-the-context-window.md); on SpeechAnalyzer while the person talks (`sortsWhileListening = true`, the finished words read every 150 ms), on SFSpeechRecognizer and for typed notes at the stop. Lines (`TodoValidation.lines`, unit tested): one per sentence; a sentence over 12 words in pieces at its commas and "and"s (a piece under 3 words, or with no content word, stays with its neighbour); a day header with its sentence; leading filler dropped; over 120 characters carried on to the next line at a space, never cut off; filler-only pieces skipped; a one-sentence note stays one to-do. `FallbackSorter` (older iPhones and iOS 17 to 25, Apple Intelligence off, or its model not ready yet) saves the note as said, in lines, with one line under the list saying why: "Saved as you said it. The transcript is read word by word (lowercase, punctuation as breaks, "p.m." as pm, "4pm" as 4 pm); a comma, ";", "and", "then", "also", "plus" and "after that" are strong separators, and a full stop, "?" or "!" only a weak one, since the transcriber may finalise "Order." on its own. Every part's tasks go through it once, together, against the whole note. G1 places each task, inside its own part's words for a long note: its `spoken` words as a run, first after the previous task, then anywhere, then fuzzily (80% of them in order, rounded up, and one miss allowed in two to four words as long as a content word is found, so "call mom" places on "call mum"; a word written two ways, "e-mail" and "email", is one); failing that, by 60% of its title's content words (50% when the model left `spoken` empty), so a `spoken` copied a little off ("I have got to renew the car tax" for "I've gotta renew the car tax") still places; a trailing s or es is ignored in title placement and in the unsaid-word check, and a kept title takes the spelling said ("LinkedIn ICP" for "LinkedIn ICPs"). A task that places neither way was never said and is dropped (the lines fallback runs only when nothing is left, so a task that was said must never be dropped); only a stored answer with no `spoken` key passes through. G2 gives every word one owner: two tasks on overlapping words are one, and so are two whose titles share a content word said only once in the note (with no other task said between them), except that across a separator or a full stop the shared word must be the action one title leads with and the other task's words hold, as in a list split into things ("buy milk", "eggs" titled "Buy eggs"); a word carried over as context ("call a plumber" titled "Call a plumber about the boiler" after "email the landlord about the boiler") leaves two actions apart. Stopwords, day and month names, today, tomorrow and tonight, time and number words and digits never count as shared, so three tasks sharing "today by 4 pm" stay three. G4 makes a note of 8 words or fewer with no strong separator one task (a weak separator counts only when both sides stand alone: 2 or more content words, or one with its own day or time, so "Order. Reach toothbrushes on Amazon." is one task and "Dentist tomorrow. Call mum." two). Neither G3 nor G4 joins tasks the model gave different due dates ("Gym at 6. Call mum at 8." stays two, with both notifications). A lone word after a full stop still joins what follows ("Order. Reach toothbrushes" and, by the same rule, "Laundry. Call mum."): telling a verb from a noun in one word needs a part-of-speech guess Apple does not document for this. G5 rebuilds a merged title, or one that is empty, adds a word never said (a leading verb is allowed), drops a name it covers, drops a word from the middle of what it kept, or leaves out the action said before it in its clause (a title starting on a word said, past articles and prepositions after a content word it left out, is rebuilt from where the action starts, at most 6 words back and never into another task; a copula ends the clause, and a word right before the verb is not taken for the action, so "I should send messages to my LinkedIn ICPs" saves "Send messages to my LinkedIn ICPs"), from the speaker's own words over its span (to the next separator or task, leading filler such as "I need to", "I should", "I'm going to", "we need to", "remember to", "um", "so", "also" and "and" stripped, the day or time phrase stripped when there is a due date, at most 8 words, never ending on a function word or a separator, and when the cut falls inside a task said after a separator that task goes whole, so "Email the landlord about the boiler" never ends "... and call"; a capital first letter). G7 (last; feedback build 2: Tom's "LinkedIn ICP" came back due at a 17:18 nobody said): a due stands only where a day or time was said for that task (its words, earlier in its sentence, up to the next break after it, a header just before, or the part's carried phrase), from a narrow list: today, tonight, tomorrow, yesterday, morning, afternoon, evening, noon, midday, midnight, weekday names (their short forms, mon to sun, only before a time or after on, by, this, next and the like), month names except "may" and "march", next week, this week, weekend, end of day, eod, and numbers only beside at, by, around, before, until, till, from, past, am, pm, o'clock, quarter to, or in "in N minutes/hours"; never now, later, soon, next, last, first or second. Otherwise the task stays and its due goes. G2 to G7 run once over the whole note, so a task split across two parts ("Order Reach toothbrushes. | On Amazon") joins again. Then titles are trimmed, list markers, quotes and end punctuation dropped, a capital first letter, at most 120 characters, duplicates (by title and due, across the whole note) and empties dropped, at most 80 sorted tasks a note (lines are never cut); due dates parsed from local ISO 8601 (with or without seconds or an offset) or a date alone, a time kept only when one was said and written, days that do not exist rejected; a clock time more than 10 minutes gone (up to a day) moves to its next occurrence, whole days in the speaker's zone ("today by 4" said at 01:30 while the day being lived is still yesterday, "call Sam at 9" said at 21:30), so no task is saved late and silent; more than a day behind or a year ahead is dropped. A model error (guardrails, a refusal, an unsupported language, a rate limit) or a part's deadline saves that part as said, in its place. Weak-linked (`-weak_framework FoundationModels`), so iOS 17 to 25 never load the framework.
- On-device eval (internal builds only, `Taken/Todo/TodoEval.swift`): CI cannot run Apple's model and a TestFlight app takes no launch arguments, so the link `taken://todo-eval`, opened from Safari on the owner's iPhone, runs 15 fixed notes one after another through the real sorter with a fixed context (Monday 19 October 2026 10:00, Europe/London, day start 04:00). Case 13 is "I should send messages to my LinkedIn leads" (1 to-do, keeps send, messages and leads, no due); 14 a 2-minute ramble (about 290 words, 10 actions, 9 to 11 to-dos, every name kept) and 15 a 5-minute ramble (about 760 words, 20 actions, 18 to 22), sorted part by part back to back as while talking, which pass only if every part takes 15 s or less. - Voice note log (feedback build 2, internal builds only, `Taken/Todo/VoiceNoteLog.swift`; Tom: "I think it'd be really helpful if you could see it"): compiled only under `#if DEBUG || TAKEN_INTERNAL`, recording and opening only with `InternalBuild.unlocksPro`, so Debug, CI and App Store builds never show it. ## 4. Design language and looks (1.1: calm, premium, minimal)

Tom on 1.0 (2026-10-13): "too mil-techy ... too many little decals ... It needs to be a lot simpler." 1.1 keeps the dark premium canvas, one accent colour, Geist, generous spacing, large tap targets, the satisfying check and the haptics, and drops the HUD decals from the app: no section numbers ([01]), no tick ruler, no dashed rules, no viewfinder brackets, no LIVE / LOCKED IN labels, no scanline, no code tags on rows, no giant counter (REDESIGN.md, "Simpler visual language"). The widgets and the app icons keep their own compact look.

Signal, the default look, is lifted from Tom's own sites badline.ai and lupolabs.ai (same token system). Three more looks are chosen in Profile > Look (3.4): one tap each, never at first launch, and a look is named for its colour, never for who might pick it.

Theme system (`Shared/Theme.swift`, compiled into the app and the widget extension):
- `ThemeID` (`signal`, `blush`, `phosphor`, `ice`) is the stored choice. `ThemeTokens` holds a look as numbers, `Theme` turns them into the colours plus the radius scale, the brackets and the glow, and is injected through the environment (`@Environment(\.theme)`). Views and widgets never name a colour of their own: `grep -rnE 'Color\((hex|red|white|\.sRGB)|Color\.(white|black|gray)|\.(white|black)\b|UIColor|0x[0-9A-Fa-f]{6}' Taken TakenWidgets Shared --include='*.swift' | grep -v Shared/Theme.swift` finds nothing (`Color.clear`, no colour at all, is the one literal left).
- Colour roles, every look: `bg`, `bgRaised`, `bgInset` (canvas, panels, insets); `fg`, `fgMuted`, `fgSubtle`, `fgFaint` (text, faint = index marks and dashed rings); `line`, `lineStrong`, `lineDash`, `tick` (hairlines, dashed rules, ruler ticks); `bracket` (HUD brackets at rest); `signal` (lit fills: discs, pips, segments, lamps, the glyph's lit half), `signalInk` (the signal as text and thin marks, AA on canvas, panels and the wash), `signalEdge`, `signalWash`, `onSignal` (the check on a lit disc); `button`, `buttonText`, `buttonLamp` (light button on the dark looks, ink button on Blush; the lamp on it is a deeper shade of the signal where the button is pale); `danger` (destructive confirms only).
- Signal tokens are 1.0's exactly: `bg #08090A`, `bgRaised #0F1012`, `bgInset #16171A`; `fg #F4F4F5`, `fgMuted #A1A1AA`, `fgSubtle #7C7C85`, `fgFaint #52525B`; `line rgba(255,255,255,0.10)`, `lineStrong rgba(255,255,255,0.18)`, `lineDash rgba(161,161,170,0.20)`, `tick rgba(161,161,170,0.34)`; `signal #FFB000`; `danger #FF5F57`. Lit = taken. Everything else is monochrome.
- Blush: canvas `#FBF6F3`, panels `#FFFDFB`, insets `#F3EBE7`; ink `#2A2224`, `#5F5357`, `#776A6E`, faint `#A5979B`; warm grey hairlines (ink at 10% and 18%); rose `#D9667F` for what is lit and `#A8425C` wherever the rose is text; white check on a lit disc; ink button with off-white type; danger `#C0392B`. Calm and premium: no gradients beyond the faint signal wash, a soft glow at 0.35 of Signal's.
- Phosphor and Ice are the Signal composition recoloured, with greys tinted toward their accent: Phosphor `#060908` / `#0C110E` / `#131915`, text `#EFF5F1` / `#9EAAA3` / `#78847D` / `#4E5852`, glow 0.85; Ice `#07090C` / `#0D1015` / `#14181E`, text `#F0F3F7` / `#A0A9B4` / `#7A838E` / `#4F5661`.
- Radius scale (`ThemeRadius`): card (the app's cards, buttons, toasts and the locked bar; history cells and fields use half), frame, panel (the widgets' panels and buttons), control (chips), mark (the widgets' PRO glyph), segment (the widgets' bars), lamp roundness. HUD (Signal, Phosphor, Ice): card 14; frame 0, panel 4, control 3, mark 2, segment 0, square lamps. Soft (Blush): card 16; 14, 12, 8, 4, 3, round lamps.
- Brackets (`ThemeBrackets`): the app icons' brackets (brand/icon.py mirrors the tokens): HUD looks 1.5 pt square viewfinder corners; Blush delicate 1 pt brackets at 0.8 of the length, as arcs that follow a rounded frame. The app itself draws none.
- Persistence: the look is `StackSettings.theme` in `taken.json`, the App Group store, so the widgets and the control read it with the stack. A file without it (1.0) or with a look this version does not know reads as Signal and loses nothing else; Erase all data keeps the look. Unit tests (`TakenTests/ThemeTests.swift`) cover decoding, the round trip, erase, the widget snapshot, the `-theme` launch argument, Signal's exact tokens, AA contrast for every look and the alternate icons in the bundle.
- Appearance: the root and every sheet set `preferredColorScheme(theme.scheme)`, so the status bar, menus, dialogs, pickers and system sheets follow the look. The launch screen stays `#08090A`; a light look fades in from it (0.4 s) instead of cutting to white.
- App icons: every look has its own icon, rendered by `brand/icon.py` from the same tokens (`--looks-sheet` writes the review sheet, `brand/looks.jpg`). Signal is the primary icon (`AppIcon`); `AppIcon-Blush` (the capsule in rose on off-white, delicate rounded brackets), `AppIcon-Phosphor` and `AppIcon-Ice` (the Signal composition recoloured) are alternate icons with dark and tinted variants, listed in `ASSETCATALOG_COMPILER_ALTERNATE_APPICON_NAMES`. Taken calls `setAlternateIconName` once Profile closes (iOS confirms with its own alert, so trying every look ends in one alert) and checks again on every return to the foreground.

Shared by every look:
- Type: Geist (sans) for every title, name, label, button and line, in sentence case; Geist Mono only for times and counts ("07:42", "4 of 6", prices). The Add sheet's chips keep their mono caps, as Tom liked them; the widgets keep mono caps labels. Both are SIL OFL fonts by Vercel: bundle the TTFs (download from the official npm package `geist` or github.com/vercel/geist-font releases) and register via UIAppFonts; include the OFL licence file. Numbers use monospaced digits.
- Surfaces: large titles, raised cards with a 1 px hairline and the look's card radius, solid hairlines between rows, generous spacing, no drop shadows except a soft signal glow on lit elements. No emoji, no illustrations, no gradients except a faint signal glow.
- Motion: snappy (0.18 to 0.3 s), a spring on the ring when an item is taken, the check springing in for All taken; a new look crossfades in 0.3 s. Respect Reduce Motion.
- Haptics: `.impact(light)` on take, `.success` notification on All taken, `.selection` on chips, menus and looks. Respect the Haptics toggle.
- Accessibility: Dynamic Type on body text (mono labels may cap at a sensible max), VoiceOver labels like "Vitamin D3, taken at 7:42 AM" / "Fish Oil, pending, double tap to mark taken" / "Blush look, selected", minimum 44 pt hit targets. Contrast AA for text in every look: `fg`, `fgMuted`, `fgSubtle`, `signalInk` and `danger` reach 4.5:1 on the canvas and on panels, `fg`, `fgMuted` and full-strength `signalInk` on the wash (the chosen plan's lines, a fully taken day's number), button type on its button; the check on a lit disc and a lit segment reach 3:1 (unit tested).
- Copy voice: short, calm, confident, sentence case. No em dashes in user-facing copy. Never the word "free". No exclamation marks.
