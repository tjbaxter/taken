## 1. The job

"It's the end of the day and I can't remember: did I take it today?"

Taken is a source of truth for a daily pill / supplement stack. Tap when taken. Look later and know for sure, with the time. Nothing else gets in the way.

Founder's words (verbatim, the bar): "Fucking frictionless. No ads, no text, no typing, literally tap, tap, tap, done." "All the apps I've seen are so complicated. You have to go through like a 20 step health funnel. It's interrupted by ads." "If I remember twice in a day, I can be like, oh, I actually did take them nine hours ago."

His stack (the first user): Vitamin D3 (daily), Finasteride (daily or every other day), Fish Oil (daily, 1 or 2 caps).

Non-negotiables:
- Zero onboarding funnel, zero accounts, zero ads, zero analytics/tracking, zero network calls of our own. Everything stays on the phone. Taken Pro (1.1) is bought through Apple's StoreKit, which talks to the App Store itself (3.9, PRO.md).
- Logging a dose = ONE tap from the app, the widget, the Lock Screen, Control Center, a notification, or Siri.
- Adding the stack needs no typing (quick-pick chips). Typing a custom name is optional.
- Opening the app answers the question in under one second: each item shows the exact time it was taken beside a filled ring, or an empty ring while pending; how long ago is one tap on the item.
- Tapping something already taken never un-takes it by accident.

## 3. Product

### 3.1 Tabs, Today and History (1.1, REDESIGN.md)
Three tabs in the system tab bar (`TabView`, Liquid Glass on iOS 26): **Today** (the stack), **To-do** (voice to-dos, 3.10) and **History** (the calendar and stats). A real launch opens on Today. The round avatar at the top right of every tab (the profile photo, else the initials, else a person glyph; 44 pt) opens Profile (3.4); there is no gear. Every sheet (Add, Profile, the item sheet, the day sheet, Set time, a to-do's edit and typing sheets) and the Taken Pro paywall are presented from the root above the tabs (`RootView`, `SheetRoute`, `.paywallHost(.root)` on the tab view), so a voice to-do request closes whatever is open and brings the To-do tab to the front (`AppModel.openTodoTab`, through `.opensTodoTab`).

Today: a large title "Today" in Geist with the date under it ("Tuesday 13 October"), then one calm progress line in doses, as the widgets count them: "4 of 6 taken" (the numbers in Geist Mono, "Last 07:44" on the right) over a slim bar. When everything due is taken the line becomes the All taken moment: a check in the look's signal springs in with the success haptic, "All taken" in the readable signal, the last dose's time and the streak. No counter panel, ruler, brackets, scanline or status labels.

Stack: the title "Stack" with "Edit" and a clear "+" beside it (Edit: Geist in the look's signal ink, 44 pt, VoiceOver "Edit stack"; "+": 44 pt, the look's signal ink, VoiceOver "Add to stack", opens Add), then a card with one row per item due today, in the user's order. Each row (Tom 2026-10-25: "They want to tap the bar"; REDESIGN.md, "Tap the row to log"):
- the name (Geist) over a small frequency line ("Daily", "2x daily · 1 of 2", "Every other day").
- on the right, the ring: empty while pending, one arc per dose for a 2x or 3x item, and once everything due is taken the look's signal with a check and the time beside it ("07:42", Geist Mono). In locked mode a small lock sits in a pending ring.
- The whole row is one button (`ItemRowView`, one SwiftUI `Button` with a `ButtonStyle`, so a scroll that starts on it never logs): the name, the frequency line, the gap, the time and the ring all count. Pressed, the row takes the flat inset highlight and the ring gives a little.
- Tap a pending item, anywhere on its row: mark taken now. The light impact haptic (the success haptic when it finishes the day), the ring fills, then lifts and settles on its spring while a soft wash of the look's signal crosses the row and fades (about 0.7 s; with Reduce Motion the wash alone, no lift; a tap only, never Take all, a widget or Siri), and a toast "Vitamin D3 taken at 07:42 · Undo" for 4 s (undo removes that dose). No confirmation and no menu.
- Tap a taken item: never untakes. It shows the detail ("Taken at 07:42 · 9h 12m ago") with a soft haptic; a second tap closes it.
- Edit beside the Stack title puts the stack in edit mode and reads "Done" (semibold) until tapped again: each row shows a chevron where the time and the ring were (they cross-fade), a tap on a row opens its item sheet (3.4) and never logs, any open detail closes and Take all steps aside. Edit mode ends on Done, on leaving Today (any tab, a voice to-do request), when Taken goes to the background and when the stack empties; closing an item sheet keeps it, so the next item is one tap away (`AppModel.editingStack`).
- Long-press the row: Undo last dose, Set time… (correct when it was really taken, or log it at that time), Edit item. VoiceOver: the row is one element whose default action is the tap ("Double tap to mark taken.", "Double tap for the times. Actions has Edit item.", in edit mode "Double tap to edit."); Edit item, Undo last dose and Set time are its actions.
- Multi-dose items (2x/3x daily): each tap fills the next arc with its own timestamp; done when all are filled.
- Items not due today (an every-other-day item taken yesterday) sit in a quiet "Not today" group under the stack, with "Every other day · Next: tomorrow" ("Next: Thursday", "Next: 21 Oct"). Its row logs a dose in the same way (no lockout), and edit mode covers it too.
- A weekly item under Not today shows the week today is in (3.3, `StackEngine.weekRow`): while its due day has nothing logged, a quiet ring that logs on tap and "Weekly · Thu · Not logged" (VoiceOver "Semaglutide, Thursday's dose not logged.", "Double tap to mark taken."); once logged, the lit ring with the time ("08:12" if logged today, "Fri 08:12" otherwise) and "Weekly · Next: Thursday", where a tap shows "Taken at Fri 08:12 · 1d 2h ago" and never logs a second dose (VoiceOver "Semaglutide, taken Friday at 8:12 AM."); on the 1 to 3 days before the next due day, with this week logged, the row is today's quiet ring with "Weekly · Next: Thursday" again, so an early dose is one tap and counts for the next due day (R1 b, `StackEngine.takesEarlyDose`); after an early dose, "Tue 08:12" and "Weekly · Counts for Thursday". Its long-press: Undo last dose removes the row's own last dose, whatever day it was logged (the toast's Undo puts it back); Set time… only when that dose was logged today, otherwise "Open Thursday" (that day's sheet; a day still to come reads as pending, never missed); Edit item. On the due day an early dose keeps its day everywhere it is shown: the row "Tue 08:12", the progress line "Last Tue 08:12", VoiceOver "Semaglutide, taken Tuesday at 8:12 AM." and Siri "Semaglutide taken Tuesday at 8:12 AM." Whether a dose was logged on another day is read as the engine reads it, at the dose's own UTC offset, so a dose logged on time abroad shows no day.

One quiet row (1.1 retention build, 3.11): under the progress line, at most one at a time and never while locked (the Pro bar sits there), Today may show the evening nudge offer ("Nudge me at 20:00 if anything's left", Turn on and Not now), the repair line ("Forgot to log yesterday? Fill it in", which opens yesterday's day sheet, with a close) or the widget invite ("Log from your Lock Screen: add the Taken widget", with a close). While notifications are not allowed for Taken and a trial's reminder moment has come, the trial notice (the reminder's own words, a tap opens Manage subscription, 3.9) comes before all three. `TodayPrompts.pick` decides, in that order; `Taken/Views/TodayPromptRow.swift` draws them in the look's tokens.

Locked mode (no Taken Pro after the first dose, 3.9): everything above stays on screen and readable, and a tap on a pending row opens the paywall as the ring did. A slim bar under the progress line reads "Start 7 days on us" (while the introductory offer applies) or "Pro ended. Tap to continue", and a tap opens the paywall. Logging a dose, Take all (it carries a small lock), Set time, backfill, adding items and turning the nudge on open the paywall instead, and the tap that opened it is applied once Pro is on. Undo, removing a dose, the item sheet, the day sheet and CSV export always work.

Take all: when 2+ items are pending and the stack is not being edited, a plain full-width "Take all" button with the count in Geist Mono sits above the tab bar, with the undo toast. One tap marks every pending due dose taken at the same time. With fewer it is hidden. Repeat guard: Take all (here and from a widget, the control, Siri or the nudge) and a widget row skip an item logged in the last hour, so a second tap never logs a 2x or 3x item's next dose on top of the first; the count on the button is what it will log. A tap on a row in the app is never held back.

History: a large title "History" ("The last five weeks"), the streak ("14 days": consecutive complete days, today counts once complete, a day with nothing due does not break it, and a weekly item's open week neither breaks nor grows it, 3.3) and the last 30 days ("97%": due doses taken / due doses) as two cards, then a 5-week grid (Mon-first, today at bottom right, outlined): a lit check where everything due was taken, a half-lit bar for some, a dim dot for none taken or none due, nothing for days to come. Tap a day: the day sheet, listing that day's items with their taken times; each item can be marked taken at a time (an hour after the wake-up time on an earlier day, now for today, adjustable) or a dose removed. This is the backfill path for "I forgot to log yesterday".

### 3.3 Add sheet (no typing needed)
A grid of quick-pick chips, exactly as 1.0 had them (multi-select, tap to toggle, mono caps): Vitamin D3, Fish Oil, Omega-3, Magnesium, Creatine, Multivitamin, Zinc, Vitamin C, Vitamin B12, Iron, Probiotic, Ashwagandha, Melatonin, Collagen, Finasteride, Minoxidil, Birth Control, Metformin, Semaglutide, Tirzepatide, Electrolytes, Vitamin K2, Folate, Calcium, Turmeric, CoQ10, L-Theanine, 27 in all (Birth Control `BCP`, Semaglutide `SEM` and Tirzepatide `TRZ` joined in the 1.1 feedback build; generic names only). Each chip carries a sensible default code tag (3 characters, unique). Plus a `+ CUSTOM` chip that opens type mode (the only place typing exists, and always optional). Target: Tom adds his whole stack in 4 taps.
- Type mode (`Taken/Views/AddSheet.swift`): `+ CUSTOM` shows its selected state and a 50 pt field ("Search or type a name", today's raised background and focus edge, a clear button while it has text, words capitalisation, no autocorrection, Done) slides in pinned under the sheet header, above the chips' ScrollView (opacity combined with a move from the top, `.snappy(duration: 0.28)`), so neither the keyboard nor the Add bar can cover it. Focus is set from the field's own `onAppear` after one main-actor hop (`defaultFocus` does not fire for a field inserted by a tap). There is no matchedGeometryEffect across the ScrollView edge (unverified there). While the field has focus the Add bar steps away and comes back when the keyboard goes; nothing animates per keystroke. A pick (a suggestion, or Return) plays the selection haptic and closes the field back into `+ CUSTOM`: a chip's name lights that chip, any other name becomes a selected chip just before `+ CUSTOM` with its How often row. Losing focus with text keeps the text as a pick, as before, and brings the Add bar back; losing focus with the field empty leaves type mode.
- Type-ahead (`Shared/Suggest.swift`, `Suggest.rank`, pure, unit tested): up to 4 matches under the field, then `+ Add "<typed text>"` last in the readable signal (left out only when the text is exactly a catalog name). 48 pt rows in a card, the typed letters muted and the rest semibold, a brand alias faint after its generic name ("Tirzepatide · Mounjaro"), an item already in the stack faint with a check and not pickable, nothing while the field is empty. Rows are capped to the height measured under the field, so on an iPhone SE with the keyboard up (about four rows) or at large text the field stays in view and nothing scrolls. VoiceOver hears "N suggestions" once typing pauses (0.4 s). Return picks the first row that can be picked and never rewrites what was typed. Matching folds case, accents and width, drops apostrophes and also compares a form without spaces (coq10 finds CoQ10, omega3 finds Omega-3). Tiers: exact, starts with, every typed word starts a word of the name (vit d, lions mane), then typos (optimal string alignment distance to a prefix of the name: none up to 3 letters, 1 from 4, 2 from 8; a first-letter typo ranks as two, Algolia's defaults). Common misspellings (tumeric, ashwaganda, magnisium, glucosomine) are hidden aliases that rank as one typo. Ties: tier, typos, chips first, the catalog's weight, a name before an alias, the shorter name, then alphabetical. No phonetics, no model.
- The bundled list (`Shared/Catalog.swift`, `Catalog.entries`, 299 names): ingredient-level vitamins and minerals with the forms people buy, botanicals, other supplements, common daily medicines by generic name (US names as RxNorm spells them, the UK name as an alias), the two GLP-1s (and Oral Semaglutide, the once-daily tablet: Rybelsus and the Wegovy pill are its aliases) and Birth Control. Spellings and brand-to-generic mappings were checked offline against RxNorm and the NIH DSLD; the app never looks a name up: there is no runtime lookup of any kind and the list ships in the binary. Brand names are aliases only, shown faint in suggestions, never as chip labels, in metadata or in screenshots. No retatrutide, CagriSema or research peptides (BPC-157, TB-500, GHK-Cu, CJC-1295, ipamorelin, MOTS-c, Semax, Epitalon, sermorelin) as entries or aliases (unit tested); anything can still be typed and added through the `+ Add` row. `Catalog.presets` (the chips) is derived from the entries, and `Catalog.code(for:)` reads names, then aliases and misspellings ("Mounjaro" is TRZ), then derives a code as before (Lion's Mane is LMN).
- How often: once something is picked, each pick has its own menu: Daily (the default), Every other day, Weekly, 2x daily, 3x daily, Days (weekday toggles under it; Days with none picked adds it as daily), so Finasteride can be every other day while D3 is daily, in one go (`AddPlan.drafts(selected:extras:choices:days:weekly:weekday:)`, unit tested: a pick that is not a chip has its own frequency and takes its catalog code when it has one, and a name picked twice is added once). "Add 3" confirms.
- Weekly: saved as a one-day Days set (`.weekdays([d])`), so the file format does not change, and a one-day set reads back as Weekly. A single-select weekday row under it starts on the weekday of the day being lived; the item sheet offers the same. It reads "Weekly · Thu", and "Weekly · Next: Thursday" under Not today. Semaglutide and Tirzepatide (and the brand names of their injections typed in full: Ozempic, Wegovy, Mounjaro, Zepbound) start on Weekly; Oral Semaglutide (Rybelsus, the Wegovy pill, typed or picked) starts on Daily, since the tablet is taken once a day; Semaglutide picked from its chip for a tablet is one switch to Daily; Birth Control starts on Daily. A weekly dose counts for its week (2026-10-21 retention build; "Weekly" here is a one-day Days set on the day in question, and Daily, 2x or 3x daily, Every other day and Days sets of 2 or more are unchanged). Taken only records doses: it never says when, whether or how much to take. With D a due day, D's week D to D+6, and N = D+7:
  - R1 Credit (`StackEngine.init`, after frequency eras, archived stretches and the start day, from `createdAt` and the first logged day): going through the item's doses in time order, each dose's logged day x is found as before, at its own UTC offset. (a) It counts for P, the latest due day on or before x (0 to 6 days back), when P is on or after the start, the item was not archived or paused on P, P has the same Weekly schedule as x, and nothing counts for P yet. (b) Otherwise, when P already has its dose, a dose 1 to 3 days before N counts for N, if N has the same schedule, is not archived and has nothing yet (the 3 days is our own choice: the smallest window that covers "a day or two early"; no label sets it and nothing on screen mentions it). (c) Otherwise it stays on x: a second dose on the shot day, an extra one mid-week, one before the first due day. Only the day a dose counts for changes: its takenAt stays the real time, and CSV export keeps the real date and time.
  - R2 Open week: D is open while nothing counts for it and today is D+1 to D+6 (`ItemDay.isOpen`, whose due count is 0), so D's status, the grid, the streak and the 30-day % neither gain nor lose by it. On D it is due and pending as before. From N on D is closed: still empty, it is missed and the streak breaks then, not on D+1. N shows one ring and one pending dose, never 2. `summary(for:asOf:)` and `sheetSummary(for:asOf:)` take the day being lived; nil turns the rule off (the nudge plan, the widgets).
  - R3 Quick paths never log a weekly item off its day: Take all (in the app, the nudge, a widget, the control, Siri) and widget rows log only today's pending rows, and an open week is never one. The item's own row, the day sheet, Set time and Siri "Log item" log it.
  - R4 The nudge does not change: one at the nudge time on D while pending, none on D+1 to D+6, none on an N an early dose already covers, and last night's nudge tapped the next morning logs nothing and opens D's sheet. How late a dose may still be taken differs by medicine (Ozempic within 5 days, https://www.novo-pi.com/ozempic.pdf; Wegovy only if the next dose is more than 2 days away, https://www.novo-pi.com/wegovy.pdf; Zepbound within 4 days, https://pi.lilly.com/us/zepbound-uspi.pdf), so a later-day prompt would be dosing advice (App Review Guideline 1.4).
  - R5 The streak, the grid and the 30-day % follow from R1 and R2. No freeze, no paid repair, no "perfect" badge, no "streak broken" message.
  - Copy: no countdown, no "still time", and none of late, overdue, days left, take it or skip on any weekly surface; "missed" never while a week is open. The day sheet reads "Weekly · Thu · not logged" for an open row and "1 of 2 taken · 1 not logged" for its day, its chip "Fri 08:12" and its removal title "Remove the Fri 08:12 dose of Semaglutide?"; Friday's sheet no longer lists that dose. Siri's "Did I take it?" says, instead of "is not due today", "Semaglutide was taken Friday at 8:12 AM, for Thursday.", "Semaglutide was taken Thursday at 8:12 AM." or "Semaglutide is not logged for Thursday." The item sheet with Weekly chosen adds one fixed line to the Schedule footer: "Missed a dose? Follow your medicine's leaflet, or ask your pharmacist or doctor." (Guideline 1.4.1). Not in this change: a Skipped button (it needs a new kind of record in taken.json).

The item sheet (on Today, Edit then a tap on the item's row, or Edit item in the row's long-press menu; or its row in Profile): Name, How often (the same menu as Add, weekday toggles for Days, one weekday for Weekly), Archive ("Hidden from Today. ### 3.5 Reminders (local notifications only)
- If enabled: at the nudge time, if any due dose for the current day is still pending, a notification `2 pending: VD3, FSH` with action buttons `TAKE ALL` (runs in background, no app open, marks all pending due doses taken now) and `OPEN`.
- Implementation must not nag when everything is done: schedule individual (non-repeating) requests for the next 14 days; cancel/reschedule today's whenever state changes (dose logged in app, widget, intent, notification action) and on every app foreground. Content lists what is pending as of scheduling, refreshed on every state change.
- Nudges follow Taken Pro (3.9): none are scheduled without it, and a subscription set not to renew is nudged only until it ends. A nudge's TAKE ALL while locked logs nothing, says `Logging needs Taken Pro. Open Taken to continue.`, and Taken offers that TAKE ALL with the paywall when it next opens.
- A weekly item is nudged on its due day only (3.3, R4): never on the six days after it while its week is open, and never on a due day an early dose already counts for, so the nudge never reads as "take another".
- Each nudge carries its logical day. Its TAKE ALL logs only on that day: last night's nudge tapped this morning logs nothing and says so. Tapping a nudge opens the app on that day. Delivered nudges for earlier days, and today's once nothing is pending, are withdrawn on every reschedule.

### 3.8 Data and correctness (code owns truth)
- Model: `Item { id: UUID, name, code, frequency (.daily(times: 1...3) | .alternateDays | .weekdays(Set<Int>), Weekly being a one-day set), createdAt, archived, archivedAt, sortIndex, pastFrequencies: [{ until, frequency }]?, pauses: [{ from, until }]? }`, `Dose { id, itemId, takenAt: Date, utcOffset: Int? }`, `StackSettings { wakeMinutes, reminderEnabled, reminderMinutes?, hapticsEnabled, theme }` with `dayStartHour` derived from the wake-up time (3.4). Day membership of a dose = calendar day of (takenAt - dayStartHour) at the UTC offset where it was logged (`utcOffset`; doses saved before it existed use the current zone). Each day uses the frequency in force that day. Everything computed from doses; no denormalized "done" flags.
- ALT DAYS: due today if never taken, or the last dose's day is 2+ days before today. If taken yesterday: not due, NEXT TOMORROW.
- Store: one Codable JSON file in the App Group container, written atomically, read/written through NSFileCoordinator so the app, widget and intents never clobber each other. A single `TakenStore` type (in a shared framework or shared source folder compiled into both targets) owns load/save/mutations and is the only writer. App reloads from disk on scenePhase .active and on a Darwin notification or file presenter callback when the widget writes.
- Timezone and DST: store absolute Dates plus the UTC offset each dose was logged at; compute today with the user's current calendar and the rollover hour, and each dose's day at its own offset, so travel never moves a dose to another day.
- Unit tests (XCTest target `TakenTests`) cover: day rollover, ALT DAYS due logic, weekday frequency, multi-dose completion, streak (incl. days with nothing due, today incomplete), 30-day %, take-all only touching pending due doses, undo, backfill on a past day, CSV export, store round-trip and concurrent writers, and a weekly dose's week (`WeeklyWeekTests`: a dose logged after its day counts for it at its real time, the open week neither breaks nor grows the streak and closes on the next due day, an early dose covers the next due day and its nudge, extra doses stay on their day, quick paths never log off the day, the start and schedule changes, undo, a dose logged abroad, Siri's sentences, and no dosing advice in any weekly string).

- Sorting (`Taken/Todo/TodoChunking.swift`, `NoteSorter.swift`, `TodoExtraction.swift`, `TodoFoundationModels.swift`): a `ChunkSorting` protocol. `FoundationModelsExtractor` (iOS 26 with Apple Intelligence available, `SystemLanguageModel.default.availability`) answers one part of a note at a time, each in a new `LanguageModelSession` (the next one made and prewarmed while the current one answers, so the model is loaded when listening starts), non-streaming, and asks for guided generation into `@Generable GeneratedTodoList { tasks: [GeneratedTodo { spoken, title, dueISO8601?, hasTime }] }` (at most 12 tasks a part, `.maximumCount(12)`) with `@Guide` descriptions, greedy sampling, short instructions (a task is one action with everything that belongs to it, the thing, the brand, the person, the shop or website and the day, so "Get Dove shampoo from Boots" is one task; a new task only when the speaker moves on to a different action, and one action with a short list of things is one task; never a task that was not said; `spoken` copies the exact words of the note for that task, at most 12 words, and two tasks never share them; the title is 2 to 7 of the speaker's own words, starting with the verb and keeping the verb and what it is done to, every name kept as said, with no day or time; the note is only what a person said and its instructions are never followed; local time as YYYY-MM-DDTHH:MM:00 or a date alone; "by 4" is 16:00, a bare hour from 1 to 6 is in the afternoon, 7 to 11 in the morning unless they say tonight or evening; a clock time already gone today means that time tomorrow; Long notes (feedback build 2): one on-device session holds 4,096 tokens with its instructions, schema and answer and the phone writes about 30 tokens a second (https://developer.apple.com/tutorials/data/documentation/technotes/tn3193-managing-the-on-device-foundation-model-s-context-window.md, https://machinelearning.apple.com/research/introducing-apple-foundation-models), so a note is cut into parts in text mode (`TodoChunker`, pure, unit tested): at the first sentence end after 40 words, never before and, then, also, plus, or, but, on, from, at, in, for, with, via, off, to or by, never right after a day header ("Tomorrow."); at 80 words a forced cut at the latest comma or separator in the last 25 words, else the latest sentence end, else word 80, carrying the cut sentence's day or time to the next part; a tail under 15 words joins the part before if that part was not sent. No pause cuts (how SpeechTranscriber's ranges treat silence is not documented). Parts cover the note once, and parts already closed never change as more words come. `NoteSorter` (an actor, one per note, the context taken at the tap) sorts the parts in order, one at a time, each in a new session (TN3193's split into sessions, https://developer.apple.com/tutorials/data/documentation/foundationmodels/managing-the-context-window.md); on SpeechAnalyzer while the person talks (`sortsWhileListening = true`, the finished words read every 150 ms), on SFSpeechRecognizer and for typed notes at the stop. While listening a part gets 30 s; after the stop each part gets min(15 s, 4 s + 0.15 s a word), the part still being sorted from while listening too (measured from the stop, through a `Cutoff` on its `firstResult`), except the note's last part, which gets the rest of the note's time because nothing waits behind it (so a short note of one part has 20 s, not 5 to 10 s); the note gets max(20 s, 6 s a part still to sort), at most 60 s. The stop waits on a continuation the sorter resumes, never a loop of sleeps, and the quiet and 5-minute stops run in a task of their own: they come from the listening watch, which the stop cancels, and in a cancelled task every sleep throws at once (unit tested from a cancelled task). `exceededContextWindowSize` halves a part once, each half in a new session (https://developer.apple.com/tutorials/data/documentation/foundationmodels/languagemodelsession/generationerror/exceededcontextwindowsize(_:).md). A failed or late part is saved as said in its place; the other parts keep their tasks, so the said order holds (`TodoValidation.assemble`). Lines (`TodoValidation.lines`, unit tested): one per sentence; a sentence over 12 words in pieces at its commas and "and"s (a piece under 3 words, or with no content word, stays with its neighbour); a day header with its sentence; leading filler dropped; over 120 characters carried on to the next line at a space, never cut off; filler-only pieces skipped; a one-sentence note stays one to-do. `FallbackSorter` (older iPhones and iOS 17 to 25, Apple Intelligence off, or its model not ready yet) saves the note as said, in lines, with one line under the list saying why: "Saved as you said it. Automatic sorting needs Apple Intelligence.", "... Turn on Apple Intelligence in Settings to sort notes into to-dos." or "... Apple Intelligence is still getting ready on this iPhone."; a note none of which was sorted reads "Saved as you said it, one line per sentence. Tap one to edit." and one sorted in part "Part of your note was saved as you said it." Code owns what is saved (`TodoValidation`, unit tested with canned model answers). First every task is checked against the words said (`Taken/Todo/TodoGrounding.swift`, `TodoGrounding.reconcile`, pure, `TodoGroundingTests`), so one action the model split is joined again (Tom's "order Reach toothbrushes on Amazon" is one task) and no task nobody said is saved. The transcript is read word by word (lowercase, punctuation as breaks, "p.m." as pm, "4pm" as 4 pm); a comma, ";", "and", "then", "also", "plus" and "after that" are strong separators, and a full stop, "?" or "!" only a weak one, since the transcriber may finalise "Order." on its own. Every part's tasks go through it once, together, against the whole note. G1 places each task, inside its own part's words for a long note: its `spoken` words as a run, first after the previous task, then anywhere, then fuzzily (80% of them in order, rounded up, and one miss allowed in two to four words as long as a content word is found, so "call mom" places on "call mum"; a word written two ways, "e-mail" and "email", is one); failing that, by 60% of its title's content words (50% when the model left `spoken` empty), so a `spoken` copied a little off ("I have got to renew the car tax" for "I've gotta renew the car tax") still places; a trailing s or es is ignored in title placement and in the unsaid-word check, and a kept title takes the spelling said ("LinkedIn ICP" for "LinkedIn ICPs"). A task that places neither way was never said and is dropped (the lines fallback runs only when nothing is left, so a task that was said must never be dropped); only a stored answer with no `spoken` key passes through. G2 gives every word one owner: two tasks on overlapping words are one, and so are two whose titles share a content word said only once in the note (with no other task said between them), except that across a separator or a full stop the shared word must be the action one title leads with and the other task's words hold, as in a list split into things ("buy milk", "eggs" titled "Buy eggs"); a word carried over as context ("call a plumber" titled "Call a plumber about the boiler" after "email the landlord about the boiler") leaves two actions apart. Stopwords, day and month names, today, tomorrow and tonight, time and number words and digits never count as shared, so three tasks sharing "today by 4 pm" stay three. G3 joins a fragment to the task it touches across nothing or a weak separator: a one-word task, or a shop, website or place said after its action, a task that leads with on, from, at, in, for, with, via or off and holds at most one content word ("on Amazon", "from Boots"), which joins the task before it. G4 makes a note of 8 words or fewer with no strong separator one task (a weak separator counts only when both sides stand alone: 2 or more content words, or one with its own day or time, so "Order. Reach toothbrushes on Amazon." is one task and "Dentist tomorrow. Call mum." two). Neither G3 nor G4 joins tasks the model gave different due dates ("Gym at 6. Call mum."): telling a verb from a noun in one word needs a part-of-speech guess Apple does not document for this. G5 rebuilds a merged title, or one that is empty, adds a word never said (a leading verb is allowed), drops a name it covers, drops a word from the middle of what it kept, or leaves out the action said before it in its clause (a title starting on a word said, past articles and prepositions after a content word it left out, is rebuilt from where the action starts, at most 6 words back and never into another task; a copula ends the clause, and a word right before the verb is not taken for the action, so "I should send messages to my LinkedIn ICPs" saves "Send messages to my LinkedIn ICPs"), from the speaker's own words over its span (to the next separator or task, leading filler such as "I need to", "I should", "I'm going to", "we need to", "remember to", "um", "so", "also" and "and" stripped, the day or time phrase stripped when there is a due date, at most 8 words, never ending on a function word or a separator, and when the cut falls inside a task said after a separator that task goes whole, so "Email the landlord about the boiler" never ends "... and call"; a capital first letter). G7 (last; feedback build 2: Tom's "LinkedIn ICP" came back due at a 17:18 nobody said; feedback build 3: one note of his saved six tasks all due 09:21, the moment he spoke, with no time said): a due stands only as far as it was said for that task, and a day and a clock time are told apart (`SaidWhen`), as date parsers do: chrono marks an hour as certain only when it was written, and Duckling gives "tomorrow" a day grain and "tomorrow at 3pm" an hour grain (https://github.com/wanasit/chrono, https://github.com/facebook/duckling). A day counts in the task's words, earlier in its sentence, up to the next break after it, in a header just before, or in the part's carried phrase. A clock time counts in narrower words: the task's own (from the break after the task before it, through its words, to the next break, separator or task), the day and time words its sentence opens with before the first task's action ("Today by 4 pm I need to email Sam, call the bank" gives both 16:00, even when the model copied "Today by 4 pm" into the first task), a header just before, or the carried phrase; never another task's words, so "Call mum at 8 tonight, take my tablets" times only mum, with or without full stops. A time is: digits or number words beside at, by, around, before, until, till, from or past (so half past and quarter past), before am, pm or o'clock, or after quarter to; a word written as one ("9:30", "4:00", "7.30", never after a currency sign); an hour after "half" (the British "half three") or before a part of the day ("8 tonight", "7 this evening", "4 in the afternoon"); noon, midday or midnight; or "in N minutes/hours", "in an hour" or "in half an hour" ("in 20 minutes take my tablets" keeps now plus 20 minutes). These follow Duckling's rules "hh:mm", "half <integer> (UK style hour-of-day)", "<time-of-day> <part-of-day>" and "in|within|after <duration>" with "a <unit-of-duration>" (https://github.com/facebook/duckling/blob/main/Duckling/Time/EN/Rules.hs, https://github.com/facebook/duckling/blob/main/Duckling/Duration/EN/Rules.hs). A day alone keeps the day and never a time ("Tomorrow call the dentist" is due tomorrow, "Ring Jo tonight" today, with no time): today, tonight, tomorrow, yesterday, morning, afternoon, evening, weekday names (their short forms, mon to sun, only before a time or after on, by, this, next and the like), month names except "may" and "march", a date ("the 23rd"), next week, this week, weekend, end of day, eod; never now, later, soon, next, last, first or second. Nothing said, and the due goes; the task always stays. A clock time within 2 minutes of the prompt's "Now" with no time said in the task's own words, a header or the carried phrase always goes (`TodoGrounding.promptNowSlack`), for an answer no word places too. Apple's TipKit lets people close a tip (https://developer.apple.com/documentation/tipkit/tips/invalidationreason/tipclosed), and Material's banner gives actions "to address (or dismiss the banner)" (https://github.com/material-components/material-components-web/blob/8d5b84fb260506c69fa93246aee538db89db8fc3/packages/mdc-banner/README.md). No freeze, no paid save, no "streak broken" message. Evidence: repair weakens the drop after a break (the Colorado study above); Silverman advises apps not to dwell on broken streaks (https://udel.edu/udaily/2024/march/power-of-streaks-motivation-jackie-silverman); a single missed day had little effect on habit formation (https://bps.org.uk/research-digest/how-form-habit).

Also in this build: "Did I take it?" requires authentication on a locked iPhone (3.7), and the age rating is 13+ (5). Order and placement of the rows (nudge offer, repair, invite; none while locked) are our own choices. `-demo` never shows a row unless the `-screen` asks: `today-nudge-offer`, `today-repair` (the demo's yesterday without its Magnesium), `today-widget-invite` (with everything taken), and `widget-help` for the sheet; CI captures all four (`RetentionTests`, `WeeklyWeekTests`).

## 4. Design language and looks (1.1: calm, premium, minimal)

Tom on 1.0 (2026-10-13): "too mil-techy ... too many little decals ... It needs to be a lot simpler." 1.1 keeps the dark premium canvas, one accent colour, Geist, generous spacing, large tap targets, the satisfying check and the haptics, and drops the HUD decals from the app: no section numbers ([01]), no tick ruler, no dashed rules, no viewfinder brackets, no LIVE / LOCKED IN labels, no scanline, no code tags on rows, no giant counter (REDESIGN.md, "Simpler visual language"). The widgets and the app icons keep their own compact look.

Signal, the default look, is lifted from Tom's own sites badline.ai and lupolabs.ai (same token system). Three more looks are chosen in Profile > Look (3.4): one tap each, never at first launch, and a look is named for its colour, never for who might pick it.

| SIGNAL (default) | `#08090A` dark | `#F4F4F5` | amber `#FFB000` | `#FFB000` | HUD |
| BLUSH | `#FBF6F3` warm off-white | ink `#2A2224` | muted rose `#D9667F` | deeper rose `#A8425C` | soft |

Theme system (`Shared/Theme.swift`, compiled into the app and the widget extension):
- `ThemeID` (`signal`, `blush`, `phosphor`, `ice`) is the stored choice. `ThemeTokens` holds a look as numbers, `Theme` turns them into the colours plus the radius scale, the brackets and the glow, and is injected through the environment (`@Environment(\.theme)`). Views and widgets never name a colour of their own: `grep -rnE 'Color\((hex|red|white|\.sRGB)|Color\.(white|black|gray)|\.(white|black)\b|UIColor|0x[0-9A-Fa-f]{6}' Taken TakenWidgets Shared --include='*.swift' | grep -v Shared/Theme.swift` finds nothing (`Color.clear`, no colour at all, is the one literal left).
- Colour roles, every look: `bg`, `bgRaised`, `bgInset` (canvas, panels, insets); `fg`, `fgMuted`, `fgSubtle`, `fgFaint` (text, faint = index marks and dashed rings); `line`, `lineStrong`, `lineDash`, `tick` (hairlines, dashed rules, ruler ticks); `bracket` (HUD brackets at rest); `signal` (lit fills: discs, pips, segments, lamps, the glyph's lit half), `signalInk` (the signal as text and thin marks, AA on canvas, panels and the wash), `signalEdge`, `signalWash`, `onSignal` (the check on a lit disc); `button`, `buttonText`, `buttonLamp` (light button on the dark looks, ink button on Blush; the lamp on it is a deeper shade of the signal where the button is pale); `danger` (destructive confirms only).
- Signal tokens are 1.0's exactly: `bg #08090A`, `bgRaised #0F1012`, `bgInset #16171A`; `fg #F4F4F5`, `fgMuted #A1A1AA`, `fgSubtle #7C7C85`, `fgFaint #52525B`; `line rgba(255,255,255,0.10)`, `lineStrong rgba(255,255,255,0.18)`, `lineDash rgba(161,161,170,0.20)`, `tick rgba(161,161,170,0.34)`; `signal #FFB000`; `danger #FF5F57`. Lit = taken. Everything else is monochrome.
- Blush: canvas `#FBF6F3`, panels `#FFFDFB`, insets `#F3EBE7`; ink `#2A2224`, `#5F5357`, `#776A6E`, faint `#A5979B`; warm grey hairlines (ink at 10% and 18%); rose `#D9667F` for what is lit and `#A8425C` wherever the rose is text; white check on a lit disc; ink button with off-white type; danger `#C0392B`. Calm and premium: no gradients beyond the faint signal wash, a soft glow at 0.35 of Signal's.
- Phosphor and Ice are the Signal composition recoloured, with greys tinted toward their accent: Phosphor `#060908` / `#0C110E` / `#131915`, text `#EFF5F1` / `#9EAAA3` / `#78847D` / `#4E5852`, glow 0.85; Ice `#07090C` / `#0D1015` / `#14181E`, text `#F0F3F7` / `#A0A9B4` / `#7A838E` / `#4F5661`.
- Radius scale (`ThemeRadius`): card (the app's cards, buttons, toasts and the locked bar; history cells and fields use half), frame, panel (the widgets' panels and buttons), control (chips), mark (the widgets' PRO glyph), segment (the widgets' bars), lamp roundness. HUD (Signal, Phosphor, Ice): card 14; frame 0, panel 4, control 3, mark 2, segment 0, square lamps. Soft (Blush): card 16; 14, 12, 8, 4, 3, round lamps.
- Brackets (`ThemeBrackets`): the app icons' brackets (brand/icon.py mirrors the tokens): HUD looks 1.5 pt square viewfinder corners; Blush delicate 1 pt brackets at 0.8 of the length, as arcs that follow a rounded frame. The app itself draws none.
- Persistence: the look is `StackSettings.theme` in `taken.json`, the App Group store, so the widgets and the control read it with the stack. A file without it (1.0) or with a look this version does not know reads as Signal and loses nothing else; Erase all data keeps the look. Unit tests (`TakenTests/ThemeTests.swift`) cover decoding, the round trip, erase, the widget snapshot, the `-theme` launch argument, Signal's exact tokens, AA contrast for every look and the alternate icons in the bundle.
- Appearance: the root and every sheet set `preferredColorScheme(theme.scheme)`, so the status bar, menus, dialogs, pickers and system sheets follow the look. The launch screen stays `#08090A`; a light look fades in from it (0.4 s) instead of cutting to white.
- App icons: every look has its own icon, rendered by `brand/icon.py` from the same tokens (`--looks-sheet` writes the review sheet, `brand/looks.jpg`). Signal is the primary icon (`AppIcon`); `AppIcon-Blush` (the capsule in rose on off-white, delicate rounded brackets), `AppIcon-Phosphor` and `AppIcon-Ice` (the Signal composition recoloured) are alternate icons with dark and tinted variants, listed in `ASSETCATALOG_COMPILER_ALTERNATE_APPICON_NAMES`. Taken calls `setAlternateIconName` once Profile closes (iOS confirms with its own alert, so trying every look ends in one alert) and checks again on every return to the foreground.

Shared by every look:
- Type: Geist (sans) for every title, name, label, button and line, in sentence case; Geist Mono only for times and counts ("07:42", "4 of 6", prices). The Add sheet's chips keep their mono caps, as Tom liked them; the widgets keep mono caps labels. Both are SIL OFL fonts by Vercel: bundle the TTFs (download from the official npm package `geist` or github.com/vercel/geist-font releases) and register via UIAppFonts; include the OFL licence file. Numbers use monospaced digits.
- Surfaces: large titles, raised cards with a 1 px hairline and the look's card radius, solid hairlines between rows, generous spacing, no drop shadows except a soft signal glow on lit elements. No emoji, no illustrations, no gradients except a faint signal glow.
- Motion: snappy (0.18 to 0.3 s), a spring on the ring when an item is taken (from a tap on its row, the ring lifts and settles while a soft wash of the signal fades across the row), the check springing in for All taken; a new look crossfades in 0.3 s. Respect Reduce Motion: no scaling or movement, fades only.
- Haptics: `.impact(light)` on take, `.success` notification on All taken, `.selection` on chips, menus and looks. Respect the Haptics toggle.
- Accessibility: Dynamic Type on body text (mono labels may cap at a sensible max), VoiceOver labels like "Vitamin D3, taken at 7:42 AM" / "Fish Oil, pending, double tap to mark taken" / "Blush look, selected", minimum 44 pt hit targets. - Copy voice: short, calm, confident, sentence case. No em dashes in user-facing copy. Never the word "free". No exclamation marks.
